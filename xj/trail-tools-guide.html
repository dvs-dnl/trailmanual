Jeep Cherokee XJ · 1984–2001

XJ Trail Tool Packing Guide

The XJ is a purpose-built trail machine that happens to have a few well-documented weak points. Pack the universal base kit, then add the XJ-specific tools for the failures that actually happen.

Base Kit — All Vehicles

These tools belong in every rig regardless of platform. If you're on the trail without them, you're relying on the kindness of strangers.

Drive Tools
  • 3/8" drive socket set — 8mm through 19mm metric, 5/16" through 3/4" SAE
  • 1/2" drive socket set — 17mm through 36mm metric (lug nuts, axle bolts), 1/2" through 1-1/4" SAE
  • Breaker bar (18" or 24") — lug nuts and stubborn fasteners
  • 3/8" drive ratchet + extensions — 3" and 6" extensions
  • Torque wrench, 1/2" drive — 20–150 ft-lb range
  • Combination wrenches — 8mm through 19mm metric, 3/8" through 3/4" SAE
  • Allen/hex key set — metric + SAE, both L-key and T-handle
Hand Tools
  • Flathead and Phillips screwdrivers — multiple sizes of each
  • Needle-nose pliers — standard pliers, channel-lock pliers
  • Diagonal cutters
  • Pry bar (18")
  • Dead-blow hammer
  • Utility knife + extra blades
  • Wire strippers/crimpers
Electrical & Repair
  • Multimeter
  • Assorted fuses — blade style, 5A through 40A
  • Electrical tape + self-fusing silicone tape
  • Crimp connectors assortment + spare wire — 14 AWG, 12 AWG
  • Zip ties — assorted sizes
  • JB Weld (two-part epoxy) — radiator cracks, threaded boss repairs
  • Hose clamps — assorted sizes, 4–8 pack
  • Duct tape
Recovery & Safety
  • Hi-Lift jack (48" minimum) + Hi-Lift base plate
  • Tow strap (20', 20,000 lb rated)
  • D-shackles (2×, 3/4" rated)
  • Tire plug kit — string plugs + insertion tool + rasp
  • Portable air compressor (12V) — capable of 35+ PSI
  • Tire pressure gauge
  • Jumper cables (20') or lithium jump starter pack
  • Fire extinguisher (ABC, 2.5 lb minimum) — vehicle-mounted
  • Headlamp + spare batteries
  • Work gloves + nitrile disposable gloves
  • First aid kit
Consumables
  • Penetrating oil — PB Blaster
  • Anti-seize compound
  • Blue Loctite (#243)
  • RTV silicone sealant — Permatex Ultra Blue or Black
  • Shop rags / blue shop towels

XJ-Specific Additions

Beyond the base kit, these tools address the XJ's specific fastener sizes, failure points, and field-repair needs.

XJ-Specific Additions
  • 10mm socket (×2) — The XJ devours them. Keep a spare or two.
  • Cam bolt tool — For caster/alignment adjustment after a hard hit
  • Oxygen sensor socket (22mm, 7/8") — The O2 sensor is a common pull-and-replace
  • Slip-joint pliers — For cooling hose clamps on the 4.0L
  • Spare 4.0L thermostat housing bolts (2) — They strip easily; carry replacements pre-threaded
Pack Strategy

Keep the 10mm sockets, fuses, electrical tape, and penetrating oil in the center console or glove box — these are the items you'll reach for first. Ratchets, sockets, and wrenches go in a tool roll. The Hi-Lift, tow strap, and compressor go in the cargo area secured so they don't shift.

The cam bolt tool and O2 sensor socket can live at the bottom of the bag — they're for when you're really in it.
