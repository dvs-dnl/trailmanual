Arizona trails, checked against your rig

Every trail here is tied to its land manager, and to a route number where one exists. Each is rated on one four-step scale, with the source ratings shown beside it, and checked against the stock tire and wheelbase we have on file for every vehicle we cover, so you can see how a stock rig compares with what the route recommends. Status is dated, and where a land manager does not list a route we say so and show the status as unknown. Confirm before you go. Plan your wheeling season with the weekend planner below.
