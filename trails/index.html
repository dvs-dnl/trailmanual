Trails

What this is

Every trail here is tied to its land manager and legal route number, rated on one four-step scale with the source ratings shown beside it, and crossed with the stock tire and wheelbase on file for every platform we cover, so you can see whether your rig clears it stock. Status is dated. Nothing here comes from a forum post alone. Plan your season with the weekend planner below.

Arizona

1 trail, each one sourced to its land manager and rated for the rigs we cover.

Broken Arrow

Difficulty 3 — Difficult 4 mi

Coconino National Forest — Red Rock Ranger District · FR 179F (spur FR 179E)

Broken Arrow is a four-mile slickrock loop over Sedona red rock with one obstacle that decides everything: The Steps, a one-way ledge descent near the end.

Plan your wheeling season

Pick a region to see its season, put trails on the weekends you want, and set your drive time to each trailhead. Then add the days to your calendar or send the plan to your crew. The plan saves on this device; a shared link sends a copy.

Written and maintained by an AZ wheeler and driveway wrencher. Trail status is re-verified against the land manager's page on a schedule; each trail page shows its last verification date.
