Table Mesa Road

Table Mesa Recreation Area, north of Phoenix (I-17 exit 236) · nearest town New River, AZ

Short answer

Table Mesa Road is the main dirt access road into BLM's Table Mesa Recreation Area north of Phoenix, entered from I-17 exit 236 and running about 4.3 to 4.5 miles, mostly washboard gravel with occasional washouts. It leads to the Little Pan staging area, dispersed camping and the area's technical routes, and BLM's route data lists it as open to all motorized vehicles.

Open as of 2026-10-30 — source. BLM's public route data lists route 9997 (about 2.2 mi in 20 segments) as an open road with all motorized vehicles allowed, under the Table Mesa travel management plan. Trails Offroad gives 9997 as the official name of Table Mesa Road West. BLM's Little Pan Staging Area page covers the recreation area but does not name the road. BLM's project page says the 2010 travel management portion is not part of the current recreation review. Confirm with Phoenix District, Hassayampa Field Office (Table Mesa Recreation Area) at 602-867-5400 before you go; status changes with weather, fire restrictions and land-manager decisions.

The trail

Table Mesa Road is the way into BLM's Table Mesa Recreation Area, which covers about 11,500 acres west of I-17 between New River and Black Canyon City. From Phoenix, BLM's directions take Interstate 17 north to exit 236 and a left on Table Mesa Road, then a right after 500 feet to stay on it. The Little Pan Staging Area is 2.5 miles up the main road, bearing left at the first fork and right at the second. It has two OHV training areas, primitive camping, two vault toilets and information kiosks.

onX measures the road at 4.3 miles and describes mostly washboard gravel with occasional washouts and ruts, wide enough for two-way traffic and fairly maintained. Trails Offroad lists 4.5 miles, about 30 minutes one way, a high point of 2,294 feet, and scattered dispersed campsites. Several reviewers found it smooth and flat, and a June 2025 reviewer took a stock Gladiator Rubicon along it. Others report campers and hikers along the edges, shooting and trash near the end of the road, and seasonal gate closures toward the river.

The road is the starting point for technical routes such as Diehard, which onX says begins at Table Mesa Road, and Raw Deal, which onX says is reached from it. It also links to connectors such as AZCO Mine Road and sits within a few miles of Lower and Upper Terminator. BLM's 2010 travel plan designated an 85-mile network of roads and trails, and its route data lists this road under number 9997 as open to all motorized vehicles. BLM's February 2024 recreation decision was rescinded in February 2025 and its project page shows the review still in progress, so shooting and camping rules in the area may change. The travel portion of the 2010 plan stays in effect.

Named obstacles

  • Washboard, washouts and ruts — onX describes mostly washboard gravel with occasional washouts and ruts. The road is wide, two-way and fairly maintained, though weather changes the surface.
  • Rough stretch near Little Grand Canyon — A September 2024 onX reviewer said the road gets rough near the Little Grand Canyon trail, with a steep hill that took a high-clearance 4x4 and a locked rear differential.
  • Gated end of the road — onX describes the road ending at a gated area where continued travel is prohibited, and reviewers report seasonal gate closures.

What this trail stresses — and what to check

Washboard

Guide: Death wobble diagnostic

Before:

  • Grab the track bar and tie-rod ends and try to move them. Any play, fix it first.

After:

  • Re-torque the track bar bolts and check tie-rod ends and ball joints; washboard loosens all three. The death wobble diagnostic covers the sequence.

Before you go

  • Land manager: Phoenix District, Hassayampa Field Office (Table Mesa Recreation Area) · 602-867-5400
  • Route: BLM route 9997 (Table Mesa Road, west of I-17), Table Mesa travel management plan. Stay on the numbered route; everything else is closed to motor vehicles.
  • Status page: blm.gov
  • Pass / permit: BLM's Little Pan page lists general public visits as free, with a special recreation permit only for commercial, competitive or large group events. Trails Offroad lists an Arizona State Land Department recreational permit as required on Table Mesa Road West and the adjoining AZCO Mine Road, which cross State Trust Land. Confirm the permit rule before you go.
  • Length: 4.5 mi — Trails Offroad lists Table Mesa Road West at 4.5 mi and onX at 4.3 mi. BLM's route data maps about 2.2 mi under the number 9997, so the rest of the road carries other route numbers. The recreation area around it holds an 85-mile designated network.
  • Season: Trails Offroad lists it as typically open year round. One onX reviewer in March 2025 found a gate toward the river closed until June 15 for bald eagle breeding season, which blocked river access, and another in February 2024 reported a closed gate to the Lake Pleasant park.
  • Cell coverage: unknown
