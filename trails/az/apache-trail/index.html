Short answer

The Apache Trail, State Route 88, is open from Apache Junction to Roosevelt Dam as of late October 2026, and the long closure many drivers remember has ended. The dirt section past Tortilla Flat reopened in 2024 after five years, and ADOT signs limit Fish Creek Hill to 4WD vehicles and UTVs with no trailers.

Restricted as of 2026-10-30 — source. The Tonto NF Canyon Lake order (updated 2026-10-23) says Apache Trail (SR 88) remains open during the lake closure, and no Forest Service alert closes the road. ADOT owns the highway and its SR 88 page was not reachable on the verification date, so the Fish Creek Hill restriction (4WD and UTVs only, no trailers, about mileposts 222 to 223.5) rests on ADOT's September 2024 reopening as reported by KTAR and KJZZ and on a tour operator's October 21, 2026 check, which says ADOT's pages disagree on whether it is in force. The July 2026 storm closure ended about 2026-07-23. Read ADOT's SR 88 banner before driving. The trail

The Apache Trail is State Route 88, a historic stagecoach route that runs from Apache Junction past Canyon Lake and Tortilla Flat to Theodore Roosevelt Dam. FunTreks logs 38.5 miles and about four hours. The first stretch is paved, narrow and curvy, with heavy traffic at times, and the pavement continues through Tortilla Flat and for several miles beyond. After that the road is graded dirt over Fish Creek Hill, then paved again near Roosevelt Dam. The Forest Service calls it a narrow, winding mountain road suitable for most vehicles.

The closure history is why many drivers think the road is shut. A 2019 storm closed the unpaved section from Fish Creek Hill east for about five years. ADOT reopened a five-mile stretch in September 2024 as a primitive road, with signs saying no trailers and 4-wheel drive and UTVs only. Monsoon storms closed the dirt section again in July 2026, and ADOT reopened it about a week later. A tour operator that checked on October 21, 2026 reports the road open end to end, with Fish Creek Hill, a steep one-lane shelf with a one-lane bridge at about mileposts 222 to 223.5, still signed for 4WD and UTVs.

Canyon Lake is closed from September 8, 2026 until it refills, under a Forest Service order that runs to February 28, 2027, but the order says SR 88 stays open. Expect no fuel on the dirt, patchy cell service and summer heat above 110 degrees. Read the ADOT SR 88 page or AZ511 on the day you go.

Named obstacles

  • Fish Creek Hill — About mileposts 222 to 223.5: a steep, narrow, one-lane dirt shelf signed Primitive Road, no trailers, 4-wheel drive and UTVs only. ADOT's own pages disagree on whether the restriction is in force, so follow the signs.
  • Fish Creek Bridge — A one-lane bridge at the bottom of the hill, where a long semi following GPS got stuck for two days in January 2026.
  • Canyon Lake closure — The lake, marina and lakebed are closed to 2027-02-28. Roadside viewing from SR 88 pullouts stays open; stay off the lakebed.
  • Tortilla Flat — Small town with a general store and restaurant, across from the closed Tortilla Campground; the last services before about 27 miles with no fuel.
  • Inspiration Point and Roosevelt Dam overlooks — Day-use overlooks on the Tonto Basin side with views of the dam and the lake; no camping or overnight parking.

FunTreks gives the road its lowest rating, which maps to Mild (1), and the Forest Service calls it suitable for most vehicles. ADOT's signs limit Fish Creek Hill to 4WD and UTVs because it is a steep, narrow primitive stretch, not because of technical obstacles, so the rating stays at 1 and the restriction is recorded in the status and notes.

Trail Manual scale: 1 Mild — graded dirt or mild two-track. 2 Moderate — rocks, ruts, mild ledges; stock 4x4 with clearance and 4LO. 3 Difficult — ledges, slickrock steps, off-camber; clearance, tires and skid protection matter. 4 Extreme — built rigs only.

What this trail stresses — and what to check

Pack against the list in the Trail tool kit builder.

Shelf road

Before:

  • Brakes: firm pedal, full reservoir, pads with life in them. Downhill traffic yields.

After:

  • Feel the rotors for heat checking; smell for cooked pads.

Steep grades

Before:

  • Shift to 4LO before the grade, not on it.

After:

  • Check transmission fluid level and smell after a long descent in gear.

Shared and one-way traffic

Before:

  • Commercial tours and the uphill rig have the right of way. GMRS or CB helps at the one-way sections.

Desert heat

Guide: Fuel range planner

Before:

  • A gallon of water per person per day is the Forest Service minimum. Check the coolant level and fan clutch before summer runs.

After:

  • Watch the temperature gauge on the drive home; a weak fan clutch shows up in traffic, not on the trail.

Before you go

  • Land manager: SR 88 through Tonto National Forest — Mesa and Tonto Basin Ranger Districts · 480-610-3300
  • Route: State Route 88 (Apache Trail National Forest Scenic Byway), Apache Junction to Theodore Roosevelt Dam; Fish Creek Hill is the signed 4WD-and-UTV stretch, about mileposts 222 to 223.5. Stay on the numbered route; everything else is closed to motor vehicles.
  • Status page: azdot.gov
  • Pass / permit: None.
  • Length: 38.5 mi — 38.5 miles per FunTreks; about 4 hours. Paved through Tortilla Flat and for several miles beyond, graded dirt over Fish Creek Hill, then paved again near Roosevelt Dam
  • Season: Open year-round, but monsoon storms close the dirt section for days at a time: damage in July 2026 closed it about six days. FunTreks prefers spring and fall. Canyon Lake and its recreation sites are closed from 2026-09-08 to 2027-02-28 for a drawdown, yet the Forest Service order says SR 88 stays open. Summer temperatures can exceed 110 degrees F.
  • Cell coverage: spotty
