White Mountains and eastern Arizona off-road trails

9 trails and about 140 route miles on file around White Mountains and eastern Arizona: 5 mild, 1 moderate, 3 difficult. None of these list a pass or permit. The latest check shows 0 listed open and 0 closed, restricted or seasonally closed; the rest have no status posted by the land manager, so we show them as unknown. Confirm before you go.

East Fork Black River (FR 276)

13 mi · Point-to-Point · Quick Run

This 13-mile forest road follows the East Fork of the Black River through a narrow valley, passing campgrounds and old Wallow Fire burn before climbing to 9,000 feet and dropping to Three Forks Road near Alpine.

FR 87, Greer to Sunrise Lake

6 mi · Point-to-Point · Quick Run

FR 87 is a 6-mile gravel forest road linking Greer with AZ-273 near Sunrise Lake, climbing above 9,000 feet through forest and wide meadows.

Terry Flat Loop (FR 8056)

9.6 mi · Lollipop · Quick Run

FR 8056 climbs 1,400 feet from US 180/191 north of Alpine to Terry Flat, a large meadow beneath 11,000-foot Escudilla Mountain, where a loop circles the meadow with spurs to views.

15 mi · Point-to-Point · Quick Run

This 15-mile route leaves Three Forks Road near Big Lake and drops through 2011 Wallow Fire burn to the West Fork of the Black River.

Red Hill Road (FR 567)

28 mi · Out-and-Back · Half-Day

Red Hill Road drops about 2,300 feet from pine forest on US 191 to the Blue River, on switchbacks with sharp drop-offs and red-rock views.

Coalson Trail #85 (FR 475)

13 mi · Out-and-Back · Full-Day

Coalson Trail #85 starts at Stacey Ranch off Forest Road 475C near Clifton and runs about 13 miles east across high grassy meadows to the Coalson cabin, where it dead-ends.

14 mi · Point-to-Point · Multi-Day

Pleasant Valley Trail #84 is Forest Road 212 along the San Francisco River north of Clifton, which the Forest Service calls an extremely hazardous road used by high-clearance 4-wheel drive vehicles and not recommended for motorized vehicles.

20 mi · Point-to-Point · Full-Day
