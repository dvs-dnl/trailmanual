Kingman and Oatman off-road trails

8 trails and about 152 route miles on file around Kingman and Oatman: 2 mild, 1 moderate, 4 difficult, 1 extreme. 3 need a pass or permit. The latest check shows 1 listed open and 0 closed, restricted or seasonally closed; the rest have no status posted by the land manager, so we show them as unknown. Confirm before you go.

Backway to Oatman

10.2 mi · Point-to-Point · Quick Run

The Backway to Oatman is a lightly used dirt route that follows the old Mohave & Milltown railroad bed from Willow Drive to Route 66, about four miles south of Oatman.

Historic

MildStatus unknown

Silver Creek Road

14 mi · Point-to-Point · Quick Run

Silver Creek Road crosses the Black Mountains on graded dirt from Route 66 north of Oatman to Bullhead City, passing old gold mines, Cathedral Rock and a wide view of the Colorado River valley.

ScenicHistoric

ModerateStatus unknown

Hualapai Mountains

33.6 mi · Point-to-Point · Full-Day

The Hualapai Mountains route is a 33-mile through trail from Hualapai Mountain Park near Kingman to paved Alamo Road at Yucca, climbing past 7,000 feet on ridge roads and dropping to the Boriana Mine ruins.

Overnight-FriendlyScenicHistoric

DifficultStatus unknown

Chloride Mines

17.8 mi · Point-to-Point · Half-Day

Chloride Mines climbs from the old mining town of Chloride past the Purcell murals into the Cerbat Mountains and out onto Big Wash Road near US-93, 17 to 18 miles depending on the source.

Overnight-FriendlyScenicHistoric

DifficultOpen

Moss Wash

9.5 mi · Point-to-Point · Half-Day

Moss Wash is a BLM-signed technical OHV trail in the Hualapai Mountains that starts in a rocky wash with deep washouts and boulders, passes the Gold King Mansion ruins, and climbs by switchbacks into pine forest.

Overnight-FriendlyScenicHistoricUTV-Legal

DifficultStatus unknown

Pass Canyon

21.4 mi · Network · Full-Day

Pass Canyon is a rugged mining-country route near Oatman and Bullhead City, about 21 miles by FunTreks and 16 by onX.

Scenic

DifficultStatus unknown

Secret Pass

17.3 mi · Point-to-Point · Full-Day

Secret Pass is a Black Mountains route between Kingman and Bullhead City, off State Route 68, with steep rocky climbs, small ledges and washouts leading to a gate on the Mount Nutt Wilderness boundary.

Scenic

ExtremeStatus unknown

Sleeping Princess

27.9 mi · Point-to-Point · Half-Day

Sleeping Princess is a BLM OHV trail that climbs about 1,600 feet up the Black Mountains above Lake Mohave in 3.2 miles of steep, off-camber, narrow and loose track with ledges and drop-offs.

ScenicUTV-Legal
