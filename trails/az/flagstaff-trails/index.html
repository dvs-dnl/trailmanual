Trails / Arizona

Flagstaff off-road trails

5 trails and about 57 route miles on file around Flagstaff: 4 mild, 1 moderate. 1 need a pass or permit. The latest check shows 3 listed open and 2 closed, restricted or seasonally closed; the rest have no status posted by the land manager, so we show them as unknown. Confirm before you go.

MildOpen

Hutch Mountain

8.4 mi · Out-and-Back · Quick Run

Hutch Mountain is a forest-road drive from Lake Mary Road up FR 135 and spur FR 135B to a 1936 lookout tower above 8,500 feet southeast of Flagstaff.

ScenicHistoric

MildClosed

Lockett Meadow

9 mi · Out-and-Back · Quick Run

Lockett Meadow is a roughly 9-mile round trip up FR 552 to a primitive campground on the northeast side of the San Francisco Peaks.

Overnight-Friendly

MildClosed

Mount Elden Lookout Road

10 mi · Out-and-Back · Quick Run

Mount Elden Lookout Road (FR 557) climbs about 2,000 feet up the west side of Mount Elden above Flagstaff on graded gravel, with switchbacks, hairpins and a gate below the summit.

Scenic

MildOpen

Schultz Pass Road

14 mi · Point-to-Point · Quick Run

Schultz Pass Road (FR 420) is a gravel road of roughly 13 to 14 miles that links Highway 180 north of Flagstaff to Highway 89 across the shoulder of the San Francisco Peaks.

ModerateOpen

Cinder Hills OHV Area

15.2 mi · Network · Quick Run

Cinder Hills is a 13,500-acre Forest Service riding area of volcanic cinders and craters northeast of Flagstaff, reached by FR 776 off Highway 89.

Overnight-FriendlyUTV-Legal

Who manages this land

Coconino National Forest — Flagstaff Ranger District; Coconino National Forest — Mogollon Rim Ranger District. Each trail page links the land manager and, where one exists, the status page and Motor Vehicle Use Map.
