Short answer

Control Road (FR 64) is a wide, graded 23-mile forest road along the base of the Mogollon Rim, linking State Route 260 near Tonto Village with State Route 87 north of Payson. Expect washboard, one-lane bridges and heavy traffic, and connections to Pyeatt Draw, Diamond Point and Houston Mesa Road.

The trail

AZ Offroad describes Control Road as a through route of about 23 miles, and the Forest Service places Forest Road 64 on the Payson Ranger District. It runs along the base of the Mogollon Rim, connecting State Route 260 and State Route 87.

From Payson, take State Route 260 east for 14.6 miles and turn left onto FR 64, which starts after a cattle guard. At about mile 1 a road to the right goes to Tonto Village. Pavement ends at mile 2, and the road continues as graded dirt heading downhill. At mile 4.3 it passes FR 65, the way to Diamond Point Lookout. FR 198 goes left at mile 5.4 toward the Pyeatt Draw trail and Houston Mesa Road. Houston Mesa Road joins at mile 13.3, and the road crosses a bridge at mile 17.5. It ends at State Route 87 at mile 23.3.

AZ Offroad lists washboard, one-lane bridges and heavy traffic, and notes numerous side roads with dispersed camping. Winter snow makes it harder. Its page dates from 2019, so bridge work may have changed some details: a 2010 Forest Service project record covers drilling at seven old bridge locations before their replacement. The guide suggests pairing it with Rim Road (FR 300) for a longer loop.

Named obstacles

  • Washboard — AZ Offroad reports washboard sections.
  • One-lane bridges — AZ Offroad lists one-lane bridges, and a Forest Service project record from 2010 covers replacing seven old bridges on this road.
  • Heavy traffic — AZ Offroad lists traffic as heavy and windy sections where drivers need to stay alert.

AZ Offroad rates it 1 of 10, which maps to Mild, and calls the road wide and graded. The Forest Service gives no rating on the pages read, so Mild (1) stands.

Trail Manual scale: 1 Mild — graded dirt or mild two-track. 2 Moderate — rocks, ruts, mild ledges; stock 4x4 with clearance and 4LO. 3 Difficult — ledges, slickrock steps, off-camber; clearance, tires and skid protection matter. 4 Extreme — built rigs only.

What this trail stresses — and what to check

Pack against the list in the Trail tool kit builder.

Washboard

Guide: Death wobble diagnostic

Before:

  • Grab the track bar and tie-rod ends and try to move them. Any play, fix it first.

After:

  • Re-torque the track bar bolts and check tie-rod ends and ball joints; washboard loosens all three. The death wobble diagnostic covers the sequence.

Shared and one-way traffic

Before:

  • Commercial tours and the uphill rig have the right of way. GMRS or CB helps at the one-way sections.

Snow and ice

Before:

  • Check the land manager's seasonal closure before you leave the pavement.

After:

  • Rinse the salt and cinders off the brake lines and frame.
