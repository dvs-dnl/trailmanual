East Valley and the Superstitions off-road trails

20 trails and about 826 route miles on file around East Valley and the Superstitions: 4 mild, 6 moderate, 7 difficult, 3 extreme. 13 need a pass or permit. The latest check shows 5 listed open and 2 closed, restricted or seasonally closed; the rest have no status posted by the land manager, so we show them as unknown. Confirm before you go.

Desert Wells OHV Area

180 mi · Network · Half-Day

Desert Wells is an Arizona State Land Department riding area near Apache Junction, off US 60 and SR 79, with more than 180 miles of signed routes across flat desert and no hill climbs.

Four Peaks Road, FR 143, is a graded dirt road from SR 87 up toward the Four Peaks saddle, about 19 miles to the trailhead and three to four hours out and back.
