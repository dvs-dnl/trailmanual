Short answer

Mount Ord Lookout is a graded 12-mile round trip up FR 626 to a locked gate below the summit tower, which most vehicles with some clearance can drive. The optional FR 27 return drops about five miles to SR 87 over washouts and needs high clearance, and reports on its north end conflict as of October 2026.

The trail

Mount Ord rises above SR 87 near Sunflower in the Mazatzal Mountains, and its road climbs from about 4,500 feet to a locked gate near the summit at around 6,750 feet. AZOffroad describes FR 626, the Mount Ord road, as a graded road of about 6 miles each way and logs it as a 12-mile round trip taking one to two hours. onX calls it a well-graded road suitable for most vehicles, and AZOffroad says most passenger cars make it to the top. The staging spot is about a mile in from the highway, past a sign reading Sycamore Creek and Mount Ord.

The gate stops vehicles short of the summit. From it a hike of roughly 0.6 mile leads to the lookout tower, where AZOffroad says the volunteer on duty may invite visitors up, with views of Roosevelt Lake and the Tonto Basin. Trails Offroad says the tower dates from 1936 and was rebuilt in 1983.

Forest Road 27 is the harder way. It leaves FR 626 around mile 4 and drops about five miles to SR 87, with washouts and tippy spots. AZOffroad rates it around 4 to 5 and says high clearance is required, with 4WD recommended. onX reports from October 2026 disagree about it: one calls the north end obstructed by washouts and off-camber ruts, another describes a filled wash and cleared trees. Treat FR 27 as optional, and check with the Mesa Ranger District, which AZOffroad lists as the contact for the area.

Named obstacles

  • Sycamore Creek and Mount Ord turnoff — A sign on SR 87 past the town of Sunflower marks FR 626; staging is about a mile in at a pull-off.
  • Locked gate near the summit — Vehicles stop at a gate about 6 miles in. A hike of roughly 0.6 mile leads to the lookout tower. Trails Offroad says to drive on to the end if the gate is open.
  • Tight turns — AZOffroad rates FR 626 1.5 of 10 strictly for some tight turns.
  • FR 27 side trip — Leaves FR 626 near mile 4 and drops about 5 miles to SR 87 with washouts and tippy spots. onX reports in October 2026 disagree: one calls the north end obstructed by off-camber washouts, another describes a filled wash and cleared trees.
  • Mount Ord fire lookout — Trails Offroad says the original tower dates from 1936 and the current one, about 102 feet tall, from 1983; communications towers share the summit.

FR 626, the route this record covers, is a graded road: AZOffroad rates it 1.5 of 10 and onX calls it well graded and suitable for most vehicles, so Mild (1). onX's 4 of 10 and AZOffroad's 4 to 5 apply to the FR 27 side trip and combined loop, which this record treats as an optional extra with its own cautions.

Trail Manual scale: 1 Mild — graded dirt or mild two-track. 2 Moderate — rocks, ruts, mild ledges; stock 4x4 with clearance and 4LO. 3 Difficult — ledges, slickrock steps, off-camber; clearance, tires and skid protection matter. 4 Extreme — built rigs only.

What this trail stresses — and what to check

Pack against the list in the Trail tool kit builder.

Steep grades

Before:

  • Shift to 4LO before the grade, not on it.

After:

  • Check transmission fluid level and smell after a long descent in gear.

Snow and ice

Before:

  • Check the land manager's seasonal closure before you leave the pavement.

After:

  • Rinse the salt and cinders off the brake lines and frame.
