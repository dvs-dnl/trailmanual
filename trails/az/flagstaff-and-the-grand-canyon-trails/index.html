Flagstaff and the Grand Canyon off-road trails

20 trails and about 921 route miles on file around Flagstaff and the Grand Canyon: 11 mild, 7 moderate, 2 difficult. 4 need a pass or permit. The latest check shows 6 listed open and 3 closed, restricted or seasonally closed; the rest have no status posted by the land manager, so we show them as unknown. Confirm before you go.

Who manages this land
