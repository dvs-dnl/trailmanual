Short answer

Upper Terminator is BLM technical vehicle route TV-3, about 0.9 mile of water-chiseled boulders, ledges, off-camber notches and tight squeezes in a desert wash in the Table Mesa Recreation Area. onX rates it 6 of 10 and says well-equipped rigs get through without body damage, reviewers bring lockers, 35 inch tires and a spotter, and BLM limits TV routes by type of vehicle.

Restricted as of 2026-10-30 — source. BLM's public route data lists TV-3 (0.87 mi in four segments) as Limited by Type of Vehicle, coded for technical high-clearance vehicles only, under the Table Mesa travel management plan, with no seasonal restriction. The line spans the same trailhead and end points Trails Offroad gives for Upper Terminator. A third-party page mentions a TV-5 on Terminator, but BLM's data checked today shows only TV-1, TV-2, TV-3, TV-4 and TV-6 in this area. BLM's Little Pan page covers the recreation area but does not name this route. Confirm with Phoenix District, Hassayampa Field Office (Table Mesa Recreation Area) at 602-867-5400 before you go; status changes with weather, fire restrictions and land-manager decisions.

Trails Offroad reviewers differ a little: one recommends a minimum 4 inch lift, at least one locker and 35 inch tires, another lockers and skid plates or sliders for 35s on a long wheelbase. Both advise against going alone and recommend a spotter. FunTreks lists lockers, a winch and tires of at least 33 inches for the Terminator route. onX says well-equipped rigs get through without body damage. BLM codes the route for technical, high-clearance vehicles only. No source gives a wheelbase limit.

The trail

Upper Terminator runs through a desert wash in the Table Mesa Recreation Area north of Phoenix, over rock the water has chiseled into boulders, ledges, off-camber notches and tight squeezes. Trails Offroad lists it at about 0.9 mile with the trailhead near 34.031, -112.188 and the route heading north, and BLM's own TV-3 line covers the same ground at 0.87 mile. The ledges keep speeds low, and drivers can pick harder or gentler lines over most of them. Trails Offroad gives about an hour for the run, and one reviewer there says to plan on at least two.

How hard it is depends on the source. onX rates it 6 of 10, says optional lines push it higher, and calls it a route that will test your vehicle and spotter. FunTreks rates the Terminator route Difficult and lists lockers, a winch and 33 inch tires as requirements. Trails Offroad reviewers recommend at least one locker, 35 inch tires, skid plates or sliders, and one suggests a 4 inch lift. Heavy runoff makes the wash much harder, per onX, so check the forecast before you go.

The BLM Hassayampa Field Office manages the land. Its public route data lists TV-3 as limited by type of vehicle, for technical high-clearance vehicles only, with no season coded. Its pages do not describe the route by name, and the Upper Terminator name comes from Trails Offroad and onX.

Named obstacles

  • Boulders in the wash — The route follows a desert wash over water-chiseled rock, with boulders spread through it.
  • Water crossings — Trails Offroad lists water crossings among the obstacles. onX says heavy runoff makes the wash much harder.
  • Off-camber notches — Notches in the rock that lean the rig sideways.
  • Tight squeezes — Narrow gaps between rock that onX says will test your vehicle and spotter.
  • Rock ledges — Many ledges that keep speeds low. Drivers can choose harder or gentler lines over them.

BLM publishes only the technical vehicle limit for TV-3, not a rating. onX rates Upper Terminator 6 of 10, which the schema maps to 3, and gives the combined Terminator 9 (4) because the lower section is the harder half. FunTreks says Difficult for both sections. Upper alone is the milder half, so it is Difficult (3), while the lockers, 35 inch tires and spotter that reviewers recommend are carried in requirements.

Trail Manual scale: 1 Mild — graded dirt or mild two-track. 2 Moderate — rocks, ruts, mild ledges; stock 4x4 with clearance and 4LO. 3 Difficult — ledges, slickrock steps, off-camber; clearance, tires and skid protection matter. 4 Extreme — built rigs only.

  • Sight down the tie rod and drag link for a bend; check toe by eye against a straight edge.

Off-camber

Before:

  • Load low and strapped. Fuel and water on the floor, not the roof.

  • Check that the roof rack and recovery gear mounts did not shift.

Water crossings

Before:

  • Walk it first. Know where your intake and axle breathers sit.

  • Check differential and transfer-case fluid for a milky look; dry the brakes before the pavement.

Before you go

  • Land manager: Phoenix District, Hassayampa Field Office (Table Mesa Recreation Area) · 602-867-5400
  • Route: BLM TV-3 (technical vehicle route, Table Mesa travel management plan); known locally as Upper Terminator. Stay on the numbered route; everything else is closed to motor vehicles.
  • Status page: blm.gov
  • Pass / permit: BLM's Little Pan page lists general public visits as free, with a special recreation permit only for commercial, competitive or large group events. Trails Offroad separately lists an Arizona State Land Department recreational permit as required on the access roads (Table Mesa Road West and AZCO Mine Road), which cross State Trust Land. No source puts a permit on the route itself, so confirm the permit rule before you go.
  • Length: 0.9 mi — About 0.9 mi: Trails Offroad lists 0.89 mi, onX 0.9 mi, and BLM maps the TV-3 line at 0.87 mi. FunTreks gives 2.1 mi for the whole Terminator run, Lower and Upper together.
  • Season: Trails Offroad lists it as typically open year-round, and BLM's route data carries no seasonal restriction for TV-3. onX says the wash is mostly dry but much harder after heavy runoff.
  • Cell coverage: unknown
