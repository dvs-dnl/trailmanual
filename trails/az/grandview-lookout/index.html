Grandview Lookout

South Rim — Kaibab National Forest, Tusayan Ranger District · nearest town Tusayan, AZ

Short answer

Grandview Lookout is a loop of graded forest road on the Tusayan Ranger District that passes a 1936 fire tower on FR 310 east of Tusayan. FunTreks measures 17 miles and about 2 hours and rates it in its lowest tier, and the Forest Service says the roads to the tower suit passenger vehicles. A longer variant adds rough FR 2718 and FR 2719.

Open as of 2026-10-30 — source. The Kaibab page for Grandview Lookout Tower shows Site Open; its last-updated date is 2025-05-20. The Kaibab alerts page retrieved today lists no closure for FR 302 or FR 310. The page does not list FR 307, FR 2718 or FR 2719. Confirm with Kaibab National Forest — Tusayan Ranger District at 928-638-2443 before you go; status changes with weather, fire restrictions and land-manager decisions.

The trail

The Grandview Lookout drive runs through forest east of Tusayan, on the Tusayan Ranger District of the Kaibab National Forest. The Forest Service gives the approach: take FR 302 east from Highway 64/180 south of Tusayan for about 15 miles to its junction with FR 310, turn left, and the tower is on your right. The tower is a 1936 Civilian Conservation Corps lookout, with a historic two-room cabin beside it. Arizona Highways says visitors can climb 80 feet of stairs for views of the Grand Canyon, the Painted Desert and the San Francisco Peaks, and that the tower is staffed in the summer fire season.

FunTreks describes a 17-mile loop of about 2 hours that passes the tower on wide, maintained dirt with a few muddy spots and ends on Highway 64 about 2 miles from Grandview Point, with side roads to explore. A 2020 update on that page calls FR 307 rutted and reports cell coverage near the tower. Arizona Highways describes a longer 30.4-mile loop out of Tusayan that adds FR 2718 and FR 2719, a rough stretch over rocky ledges and deep gullies where monsoon mud and puddles collect. It calls for high clearance there and four-wheel drive, and notes passenger cars can finish via paved SR 64.

FunTreks reports free dispersed camping in the forest. The Forest Service page shows the tower site open and was last updated May 20, 2025. Call the Tusayan Ranger District at 928-638-2443 for current road conditions.

Named obstacles

  • Grandview Lookout Tower — A 1936 Civilian Conservation Corps lookout on FR 310. Arizona Highways says visitors can climb 80 feet of stairs, and the tower is staffed during the summer fire season.
  • Rutted FR 307 — A 2020 FunTreks update calls FR 307 east of the tower rutted with little scenery.
  • FR 2718 and FR 2719 on the longer loop — Arizona Highways calls FR 2719 a rough trek over rocky ledges and around deep gullies, with mud and deep puddles in monsoon season, and calls for high clearance there.
  • Muddy spots — FunTreks reports wide maintained dirt with a few muddy spots.

Mud

Before:

  • Recovery points front and rear, rated, with a strap you can reach.

After:

  • Wash the undercarriage and radiator fins; mud holds heat and moisture against steel.

Before you go

  • Land manager: Kaibab National Forest — Tusayan Ranger District · 928-638-2443
  • Route: FR 310 (Coconino Rim Road) at the tower, reached by FR 302; guides add FR 307, FR 2718 and FR 2719 for loop variants. Stay on the numbered route; everything else is closed to motor vehicles.
  • Status page: fs.usda.gov
  • Pass / permit: None.
  • Length: 17 mi — 17 miles and about 2 hours per FunTreks; Arizona Highways describes a longer 30.4-mile loop from Tusayan on FR 302, FR 310, FR 2718 and FR 2719
  • Season: The Forest Service page (last updated 2025-05-20) shows the tower site open and states no closure season. FunTreks lists summer as the best time. Arizona Highways says monsoon rain brings mud and deep puddles on FR 2718 and FR 2719, and that the tower is staffed during the summer fire season.
  • Cell coverage: spotty
