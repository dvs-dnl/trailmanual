Sedona and the Verde Valley off-road trails

17 trails and about 202 route miles on file around Sedona and the Verde Valley: 4 mild, 7 moderate, 5 difficult, 1 extreme. 3 need a pass or permit. The latest check shows 6 listed open and 6 closed, restricted or seasonally closed; the rest have no status posted by the land manager, so we show them as unknown. Confirm before you go.

Most pins sit around the nearest town and are spread so each one can be tapped. Those do not mark trailheads, so navigate with the land manager's map. The 2 with a route map are pinned at the start of the route, and their guides have PDF and GPX downloads.

Desert Canyon Loop Scenic Drive

22 mi · Loop · Half-Day

Desert Canyon Loop is a Coconino National Forest scenic drive of about 22 miles in the Verde Valley: 13 miles of graveled FR 618 along the base of the Mogollon Rim, then 9 paved miles on FH 9 back to I-17.

ScenicHistoric

Perkinsville Road

47 mi · Point-to-Point · Half-Day

Perkinsville Road is the 47-mile Forest Service scenic drive from Williams to Jerome through ponderosa pine, ranch country and the Verde River at Perkinsville.

ScenicHistoric

MildOpen

Ranch Loop

6.5 mi · Loop · Quick Run

Ranch Loop is a 6.5-mile loop of FR 525, 761B, 761 and 525A west of Sedona that the Forest Service and Arizona State Parks describe as a quiet, sandy trail with several dry wash crossings.

+ Add to my season
MildRestricted

Salt Mine Road to Beasley Flat

20 mi · Out-and-Back · Quick Run

Salt Mine Road to Beasley Flat is a Forest Service scenic drive of about 20 miles round trip out of Camp Verde, mostly paved, ending on a half mile of gravel at a Verde River day-use site.

ScenicHistoric

+ Add to my season
ModerateClosed

Diamondback Gulch

6 mi · Loop · Quick Run

Diamondback Gulch, also called Greasy Spoon, is a 6-mile Forest Service loop of FR 152A and FR 9513 in the Red Rock Ranger District, rated Moderate by onX and suited to most high-clearance 4x4s by the land manager.

Scenic

+ Add to my season
ModerateStatus unknown

Mingus Mountain

19 mi · Point-to-Point · Half-Day

Mingus Mountain is a 17 to 19 mile route from Mingus Avenue at Cottonwood up FR 493 and FR 413 to Highway 89A near the Mingus summit, a gravel climb of more than 3,000 feet that Arizona State Parks lists as a bypass around Jerome.

Historic

+ Add to my season
ModerateOpen

Outlaw OHV Route

10.5 mi · Loop · Quick Run

The Outlaw OHV Route is a Forest Service loop on a red rock mesa west of Sedona, with trailheads at the Honanki Heritage Site and a staging area on FR 525.

Scenic

+ Add to my season
ModerateStatus unknown

Powerline OHV Trail

6.5 mi · Point-to-Point · Quick Run

The Powerline OHV Trail, FR 9524, is a 6.5-mile route in the Red Rock Ranger District that follows a power line corridor over steep, rocky ups and downs with a few difficult rock shelves.

ScenicRemote

+ Add to my season
ModerateRestricted

Schnebly Hill Road

11 mi · Point-to-Point · Half-Day

Schnebly Hill Road is an 11-mile Forest Service road that climbs from Sedona toward I-17 past Merry-Go-Round Rock and Schnebly Hill Vista.

ScenicCommercial Tours

Van Deren Cabin

5 mi · Out-and-Back · Quick Run

Van Deren Cabin is a short dirt road off Dry Creek Road northwest of Sedona, reached from the Vultee Arch FR 152 area and named FR 9917 by Arizona State Parks, that ends at a parking area a few hundred feet from the cabin.

+ Add to my season
ModerateClosed

Vultee Arch Road

4 mi · Out-and-Back · Quick Run

Vultee Arch Road, FR 152, is a four-mile dead-end road off Dry Creek Road that the Forest Service calls moderately demanding, with several rock shelves and ledges.

Commercial Tours

Broken Arrow

4 mi · Lollipop · Quick Run

Broken Arrow is a four-mile slickrock loop over Sedona red rock with one obstacle that decides everything: The Steps, a one-way ledge descent near the end.

ScenicCommercial Tours

+ Add to my season
DifficultSeasonal closure

Casner Mountain Trail #8

6.9 mi · Point-to-Point · Half-Day

Casner Mountain Trail #8 is a 6.9-mile ridge route from near FR 525 up to FR 538B that the Forest Service calls very rugged, with a narrow ridge, limited room for two-way traffic, switchbacks and a shelf road.

ScenicRemote

DifficultOpen

Cliffhanger

5.5 mi · Out-and-Back · Half-Day

Cliffhanger is a 5.5-mile one-way descent in the Red Rock Ranger District, FR 9845 and its spurs, that ends at a short walk to the old homestead on Oak Creek.

Scenic

Smiley Rock

21.9 mi · Point-to-Point · Half-Day

Smiley Rock is a roughly 22-mile point-to-point route through Prescott National Forest near Jerome, named for a rock formation and best known for a rock garden near the far end with boulders up to about two feet.

+ Add to my season
DifficultOpen

Soldier Pass

2.5 mi · Out-and-Back · Quick Run

Soldier Pass is a short, permit-only rock road above Sedona, listed by the Forest Service at 2.5 miles round trip, with ledges, slickrock steps and a narrow line to the Seven Sacred Pools and Devil's Kitchen.

Scenic

+ Add to my season
ExtremeStatus unknown

Smasher Canyon (Motorized Trail #88)

3.5 mi · Loop · Half-Day

Smasher Canyon, Motorized Trail #88, is a 3.5-mile one-way rock canyon in the Red Rock Ranger District that the Forest Service describes as extremely difficult and expert-only.

Rock Crawling

Who manages this land
