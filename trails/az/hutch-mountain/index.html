Short answer

Hutch Mountain is a forest-road drive from Lake Mary Road up FR 135 and spur FR 135B to a 1936 lookout tower above 8,500 feet southeast of Flagstaff. Arizona Highways calls for high clearance, onX rates it 1 of 10, and the Forest Service lists FR 135 as open as of October 2026.

Open as of 2026-10-30 — source. Mogollon Rim section of the Forest Roads Status table (north of Hwy 87) lists FR 135 (Hutch Mountain) as Open; page last updated 2026-10-22. The page does not list the FR 135B spur separately. The Coconino alerts page retrieved today has no alert for this road. Confirm with Coconino National Forest — Mogollon Rim Ranger District at 928-477-2255 before you go; status changes with weather, fire restrictions and land-manager decisions.

The trail

Hutch Mountain sits southeast of Flagstaff in the Mogollon Rim Ranger District of the Coconino National Forest. Arizona Highways starts the drive on Lake Mary Road about 33 miles from Flagstaff, turning east onto FR 135. After 2.6 miles a left onto FR 135B climbs the final 1.6 miles to the summit, where a lookout tower built by the Civilian Conservation Corps in 1936 stands above 8,500 feet. If a lookout is on duty you may be able to climb it with their permission. Views take in the San Francisco Peaks, Bill Williams Mountain, the Painted Desert and the Mazatzal Mountains on a clear day.

The magazine describes FR 135 as rocky and rutted but suitable for most SUVs, calls for a high-clearance vehicle, and recommends four-wheel drive on FR 135B, where snow drifts can linger on the north-facing stretch. onX rates the road 1 of 10, calls it well maintained dirt, and warns that summer monsoon rain can make it slick.

From the summit you can return the way you came, or the magazine describes continuing on FR 135 for 3.8 miles to FR 294 and following FR 294 for 5.1 miles back to Lake Mary Road, a through route of about 14 miles from the highway turnoff. onX says the road is typically open from late spring to autumn and closed by snow in winter. The Forest Service status page, updated October 22, 2026, lists FR 135 as open. Call the Mogollon Rim Ranger District at 928-477-2255 for current conditions.

Named obstacles

  • Rocky, rutted FR 135 — Arizona Highways describes FR 135 as rocky and rutted but suitable for most SUVs.
  • Steeper FR 135B spur — The final 1.6 miles to the summit are steeper, with a north-facing stretch where snow drifts linger. Arizona Highways recommends four-wheel drive here.
  • Hutch Mountain lookout tower — The summit is over 8,500 feet and holds a 1936 Civilian Conservation Corps lookout tower, which may be climbed with the permission of a lookout on duty.
  • Monsoon mud — onX reports that summer rain can make the dirt road slick.

onX rates the road 1 of 10, which maps to Mild. Arizona Highways describes a rocky, rutted road that needs high clearance, with four-wheel drive recommended only for the steeper final spur, which fits a stock 4x4 with clearance. The Forest Service lists the road as open and gives no rating.

Trail Manual scale: 1 Mild — graded dirt or mild two-track. 2 Moderate — rocks, ruts, mild ledges; stock 4x4 with clearance and 4LO. 3 Difficult — ledges, slickrock steps, off-camber; clearance, tires and skid protection matter. 4 Extreme — built rigs only.

Before you go

  • Land manager: Coconino National Forest — Mogollon Rim Ranger District · 928-477-2255
  • Route: FR 135 (Hutch Mountain); spur FR 135B to the summit lookout per Arizona Highways. Stay on the numbered route; everything else is closed to motor vehicles.
  • Status page: fs.usda.gov
  • Pass / permit: None.
  • Length: 8.4 mi — about 8.4 miles round trip to the lookout: Arizona Highways gives 2.6 miles on FR 135 plus 1.6 miles on FR 135B each way, while onX measures FR 135 at 3.8 miles; continuing on FR 135 and FR 294 back to Lake Mary Road makes a through route of about 14 miles from the highway turnoff
  • Season: onX says the road is typically open from late spring to autumn and closed in winter by snow. Arizona Highways notes that snow drifts can linger on the north-facing stretch of FR 135B. Summer monsoon rain can turn the surface to slick mud, and onX advises four-wheel drive when it is muddy. The Forest Service status page (updated 2026-10-22) lists FR 135 as open.
  • Cell coverage: unknown
