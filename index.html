Why this exists

Forums age out

The answer you need is on page 7 of a 2011 thread, behind three wrong answers and a follow-up asking if anyone ever figured it out. Trail Manual guides are built around the question, not the conversation around it.

Honest about what’s worth it

Guides are built on real owner feedback: people who did the job, broke the part, or paid too much for the wrong one. When something is overpriced or not worth it for most builds, the guide says so.

Shop manuals skip the money part

Factory manuals give you the torque spec. They don’t tell you the OEM bracket costs $280, the good aftermarket one costs $65, and the cheap one fails in two seasons. Every guide carries a price range and links to its sources.
