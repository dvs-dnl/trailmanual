The XJ database assumes you know which end of a torque wrench to hold. The Workshop doesn't — and that's not a knock. Everyone started somewhere.

These guides exist because the most common reasons a DIY job goes wrong have nothing to do with the specific repair: wrong jack placement, a breaker bar that slips, a chemical that strips paint, or a fastener torqued to spec on a cold engine. The Workshop covers the layer underneath the build.
