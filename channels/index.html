Channels worth watching.

Curated for accuracy first, not follower count. Working mechanics, serious builders, and people who've actually done the job.

📺 How this works: When a Trail Manual guide links to a video, the creator still gets the view, the ad revenue, and the credit. We embed — we don't host. Every embed includes a visible byline so you know whose work you're watching.
Jeep & Off-Road

Vehicle-specific channels for XJ, JK, TJ, JL, and trail prep.

MW

Major Weakness

Jeep XJ & MJ focused

One of the deepest XJ-specific channels on the platform. Covers Cherokee and Comanche MJ mods, repairs, and trail prep without dumbing anything down.

XJ suspension, axles, body work, trail builds

Watch on YouTube
TR

Trail Recon

Jeep Wrangler & overland

Serious overland adventures across California, Utah, Colorado, and Baja. Strong trail footage and honest documentation of what breaks and why.

JK/JL trail prep, recovery, overlanding context

Watch on YouTube
4W

Four Wheeler Network

JP Magazine / professional builds

Professional production, technically accurate builds and installs. Long-form documentation of real suspension lifts, axle swaps, and trail runs.

Technical installs, build documentation, long-form how-tos

General DIY — Accuracy First

These channels prioritize getting it right. The vehicles aren't always Jeeps — but torque specs, brake bleeding, and electrical diagnosis work the same on anything.

SM

South Main Auto Repair LLC

Eric O — 22+ years, real shop

Eric O runs a three-bay shop in upstate New York and films his actual day-to-day work. No staging, no drama — just proper diagnostic process on real vehicles.

Diagnostics, electrical, scan tool work, sensor troubleshooting

Watch on YouTube
CF

ChrisFix

Step-by-step, beginner-friendly

Best camera angles and clearest labeling of any beginner-level channel. If you need to see the exact motion — where the socket goes, which way to turn — this is the one.

First repairs, basic maintenance, fluid work, brakes

Watch on YouTube
RW

Ratchets And Wrenches

DIY focused, community-minded

Solid DIY tutorials with a tone that doesn't assume you already know everything. Honest about what's hard and what's genuinely manageable at home.

Maintenance guides, budget repairs, home garage work

Watch on YouTube
HM

Humble Mechanic

Charles — former VW master tech

VW/Audi specialist, but his content on tools, technique, and shop workflow applies everywhere. Excellent on torque sequences, thread repair, and working methodically.

Technique fundamentals, tool selection, working in tight spaces

Watch on YouTube
SD

Super Mario Diagnostics

Professional diagnostics & scan tools

Focused on the diagnostic process — OBD2, scan tool interpretation, electrical fault finding. Bridges the gap between reading a code and understanding what caused it.

Scan tool use, OBD2 codes, electrical diagnosis

Watch on YouTube

Performance builds & racing

Performance mods, build documentation, engine work

Jessicann And You Can Too!

Jessica Chou — basic maintenance

Quick techniques, tool intros, maintenance overviews
