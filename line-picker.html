Trail Manual · Line Picker

Pick a line over a ledge

Find the hazards, place your own line, drive it in three decisions, then swap the vehicle and see what changes. The obstacle is a V-notch ledge climb, and every score comes from the same height map that draws it.

Written and maintained by an AZ wheeler and driveway wrencher · Updated October 2026

    Ground is shown under the low point. Tires sit to either side of it.

    How the scoring works

    The tool samples your line every 4 inches and places the vehicle at each sample. It reads the height under each tire and each low point, and reports four things: whether the tires are up on high ground, how much room each low point has, how far the vehicle leans, and how to carry momentum. A low point fails when the ground under it reaches it. Lean warns above 25% and fails above 35%.

    Both vehicles are archetypes built to show the effect of axle type and clearance. They are not specs for any model, so measure your own low points before you use these numbers.

    What the tags mean

    CurriculumPublished four-wheeling guidance. Here, Tread Lightly's tips.
    PhysicsGeometry you can check from the numbers on screen.
    OwnerWhat experienced owners report. No training body or manufacturer is behind it.

    Sources

    1. Tread Lightly, Quick Tips for Responsible Four Wheeling: scout on foot, know your lowest point.
    2. Jeep owner driving tips: put the largest rocks under the tires, stop and ease up to an obstacle.
    3. Off Road Xtreme, with an Eaton product engineer: tires on the tall points, not over them.
