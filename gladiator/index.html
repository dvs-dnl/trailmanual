Jeep Gladiator JT · 2020–Present

Wrangler.
Bed.
Lockers.

The Gladiator is the Wrangler they should have built in 1997 — solid Dana 44 axles front and rear, removable top and doors, and a pickup bed that actually hauls. The Rubicon has factory lockers and a sway-bar disconnect. The Mojave has Fox 2.5-inch internal bypass shocks for desert running. It's a real truck and a real trail rig at the same time.
