Stock baselines

Start from your own rig. These are the factory numbers and the stock findings in the log.

  • Jeep's 2020 press kit describes the rear as a five-link coil design exclusive to Gladiator: two upper and two lower forged steel control arms plus a track bar, with coil springs and forward-facing rear shocks. The JT has no rear leaf springs. Five-link coil rear Official record Stellantis Media (2020 Gladiator press kit), 2019
  • Jeep's press kit calls the front a five-link coil setup with a forged steel full-width track bar controlling lateral axle movement. Five-link coil front Official record Stellantis Media (2020 Gladiator press kit), 2019
  • Launch press kit lists 11.1 in ground clearance, 43.6 degree approach, 20.3 degree breakover and 26 degree departure in its capability list. The sentence does not tie the figures to one trim, though the list sits beside Rubicon equipment. 11.1 in ground clearance Official record Stellantis Media (2020 Gladiator press kit), 2019
  • Launch press kit gives up to 7,650 lb towing with the available Max Towing Package and up to 1,600 lb payload. 7,650 lb tow / 1,600 lb payload Official record Stellantis Media (2020 Gladiator press kit), 2019
  • 2024 release gives ground clearance up to 11.6 in, up to 7,700 lb max towing and up to 1,725 lb max payload across the range. Top figures do not apply to every trim. Up to 11.6 in / 7,700 lb / 1,725 lb Official record Stellantis Media (2024 Gladiator release), 2023-09-13
  • Fox News test drive repeats the five-link coil description and says the setup came from the Ram 1500, with forward-facing shocks to manage loads in the bed. Used only as a cross-check on the coil rear. Five-link coil rear Reviewer opinion Fox News test drive

CV joints and front geometry

An independent front end changes CV and control arm angles as it lifts, so these findings matter more than the lift height.

  • Owner says the seller told him no driveshaft change was needed, but on his first obstacles the front driveshaft boot tore on the exhaust pipe. He replaced it with a Rough Country CV shaft. Front shaft boot tore on exhaust Owner report Quadratec reviews, 2020-03-16

Track bar and axle offset

A taller spring moves the axle sideways on its track bar. Some kits include an adjustable bar to re-center it.
