Ford Bronco II · 1984–1990

Bronco II Trail Tool Packing Guide

The Bronco II's Twin Traction Beam front suspension is capable and fragile in equal measure. The ball joints that make it work are the same ones that fail without warning. Pack to that reality.

Bronco II Tool Additions

The base kit below handles any rig. The Bronco II adds tools that address the TTB front suspension directly — specifically the upper ball joint, which is the most consequential failure point on the platform. Without the C-clamp setup on board, an upper joint failure is a flatbed call. With it, you're back on the trail in under an hour.

Ford Bronco II — 1984–1990 Additions
  • TTB ball joint press setup — A large C-clamp and receiver tube set handles field upper joint replacement; the lower requires a hydraulic press (not a trail repair). Carry the C-clamp setup for upper joints.
  • 36mm axle nut socket — Required for hub removal; this size is Bronco II specific and will not be in a standard socket set
  • Vacuum cap plugs — The TTB has vacuum lines throughout; cap plugs let you isolate and bypass failed sections without destroying the system
  • 8mm, 10mm, 13mm sockets — Everything on the Bronco II is metric; these three sizes cover most of what you'll reach for
Pack Strategy

The C-clamp ball joint setup is the most important thing on this list — it's what lets you replace an upper TTB ball joint on trail instead of calling for a flatbed. Keep it accessible, not buried under gear. The vacuum cap plugs are small and should live in your electrical kit alongside the fuse assortment.

Base Kit — All Vehicles

This is the foundation that goes into any trail rig regardless of platform. The Bronco II tool additions above layer on top of this.

Drive Tools
  • 3/8" drive socket set — 8mm–19mm metric, 5/16"–3/4" SAE
  • 1/2" drive socket set — 17mm–36mm metric, 1/2"–1-1/4" SAE
  • Breaker bar — 18" or 24"
  • 3/8" ratchet + extensions — 3" and 6"
  • Torque wrench, 1/2" drive — 20–150 ft-lb
  • Combination wrenches — 8–19mm metric + 3/8"–3/4" SAE
  • Allen/hex key set — metric + SAE
Hand Tools
  • Flathead and Phillips screwdrivers — multiple sizes
  • Needle-nose, standard, channel-lock pliers
  • Diagonal cutters
  • Pry bar — 18"
  • Dead-blow hammer
  • Utility knife + blades
  • Wire strippers/crimpers
Electrical & Repair
  • Multimeter
  • Fuses, blade — 5A–40A assortment
  • Electrical tape + self-fusing silicone tape
  • Crimp connectors + wire — 14 AWG, 12 AWG
  • Zip ties
  • JB Weld
  • Hose clamps — 4–8 pack assortment
  • Duct tape
Recovery & Safety
  • Hi-Lift 48" + base plate
  • Tow strap — 20', 20,000 lb
  • D-shackles — 2x 3/4"
  • Tire plug kit
  • Air compressor, 12V — 35+ PSI
  • Tire gauge
  • Jumper cables or jump starter
  • Fire extinguisher, ABC — 2.5 lb
  • Headlamp + batteries
  • Work gloves + nitrile
  • First aid kit
Consumables
  • PB Blaster
  • Anti-seize
  • Blue Loctite #243
  • RTV silicone
  • Shop rags
