Ford F-150 Raptor · 2010–Present

Desert running.
Factory built.

The Raptor was the first factory truck built from the ground up for high-speed desert running — not modified for it. Fox internal-bypass shocks, 35" tires standard, and an EcoBoost that pulls hard at altitude. Three generations, one mission.
