Jeep Wrangler JL Unlimited Lift Kits Compared

Jeep rates the four-door Rubicon at an RTI of 770 with the front sway bar disconnected, on 10.8 in of ground clearance Manufacturer claim. Owners of the Mopar 2 in kit report as much as almost 4 in of lift Owner report. In TrailRecon's six-kit test the Rock Krawler 3.5 in kit scored best in articulation but gained less ride height than the 2.5 in kits Measured (independent test), while AEV's 2.5 in kit gained as much or more height than the 3.5 in kits, with articulation at the back of the pack Measured (independent test). Every JL CTI number in this log comes from MetalCloak Manufacturer claim, and this page lists 184 kits from 26 brands alphabetically without ranking them.

Track bar and axle offset

A taller spring moves the axle sideways on its track bar. Owners report the offset in the kits below, and some kits include an adjustable bar to re-center it.

  • Recall 18V-675: front track bar bracket welds may be off-seam and the bracket can separate from the frame, reducing steering response. Remedy is to inspect and repair the welds. Recall 18V-675; 18,055 vehicles Official record NHTSA Part 573 Recall Report 18V-675, 2018-09
  • FCA's CTO said steering-vibration complaints involved about 2% of roughly 370,000 JLs built through June 2019, caused by air entering the original damper in cold weather. Quadratec lists the track bar, tie rods, ball joints, control arms, bushings and wheel bearings as parts to check. ~2% of ~370,000 JLs Manufacturer claim Jeep's fix for JL death wobble issue (Quadratec), 2019-08
  • TSB 08-003-21 'Improved Steering Feel' replaces the steering gear (and the track bar on JLs built on or before Jan 22, 2019) for wander, pull and excess play. Professionally lifted vehicles are eligible only if within service alignment specs, and the TSB will not fix a sustained shake or shimmy. RTI and CTI explained

RTI: Ramp Travel Index

Drive one front tire up a ramp until another tire starts to lift, then score the distance against wheelbase. A steeper ramp gives a different score, so compare only numbers from the same ramp angle.

  • RTI = distance driven up the ramp (leading edge to hub center, before any wheel lifts) divided by wheelbase, times 1,000. The standard ramp is 20 degrees; some events use a steeper 23-degree ramp for very flexible vehicles. 20° standard; 23° for high-flex rigs Reviewer opinion The RTI Ramp (JeepFan.com), 2007-06
  • Wikipedia describes RTI as distance up the ramp divided by wheelbase times 1000; ramps range from 15 to 30 degrees and 20 degrees is usually the standard, so scores from different ramp angles are not directly comparable. (d / wheelbase) x 1000 Reviewer opinion Ramp travel index (Wikipedia)

CTI: Corner Travel Index

CTI is MetalCloak's method, and every JL CTI number in this log comes from MetalCloak, including the scores it lists for competing kits. The log holds no independent JL CTI figure.

  • MetalCloak defines CTI (Corner Travel Index) as a measure of maximum axle/wheel articulation at the four corners: add the articulated wheel travel of each tire in inches and multiply by 10. It says RTI scores cannot be used to predict CTI. CTI = sum of 4 corner travels (in) x 10 Manufacturer claim Updated! Corner Travel Index (CTI) - What's That? (MetalCloak), 2023-04
  • US patent US10539484B2 (inventors Powell, Colacchia, Martin) defines CTI: tires at about 15 psi, raise one diagonal pair until the other pair is about to lift, measure each raised tire's clearance, repeat for the other diagonal. Complete CTI = (FPT+RDT+FDT+RPT) x 10; estimated CTI = (FPT+RDT) x 2 x 10. Complete CTI = 4 corners x 10; estimated = 2 corners x 2 x 10 Official record US10539484B2 - Systems and methods for measuring a corner travel index (Google Patents)
  • 4 Wheels & A Motor says CTI measures maximum travel at each corner, with tires at 15 psi, and gives 'estimated' (two-wheel) and 'certified' (four-corner) formulas. A reader comment by Matson Breakey says his MetalCloak team developed the method. Estimated: (FPT+RDT) x 10 x 2; Certified: (FPT+RDT+FDT+RPT) x 10 Reviewer opinion Ask 4WAAM - Corner Travel Index (CTI), 2023-07
