Front Dash + Door Speaker Upgrade

Difficulty 2/52–5 hrs$80–8001984-1990, 1991-1995, 1996, 1997-2001

Stock XJ front speakers: 3.5" in dash, 6.5" in front door (97-01 Country/Sport). Upgrade to a coaxial 6.5" set (Kicker, JBL, Polk) plus 3.5" tweeters or a 2-way component set.

XJ factory speaker locations:

  • Dash: 3.5" round in driver and passenger upper dash (paper cone tweeters, 4 ohm, dead within 5 years).
  • Front doors (1997-2001 Country/Limited/Sport with premium audio): 6.5" in the lower door panel. Pre-97 had no door speakers — the dash.
  • Rear: cargo-area side panels (see rear speaker entry).

Replacement options:

Budget ($80-150 total): Kicker DSC65 6.5" + Kicker DSC35 3.5" or Polk DB652/DB351. Coaxial design, direct fit, no crossover. Bolts in.

Mid ($200-400): Polk DB6502 or JBL GTO629 component 6.5" set (woofer + separate tweeter + crossover). Mount the included tweeter in the dash where the 3.5" was — most XJ installs use a Metra adapter ring.

Premium ($400-800): Focal Performance Access PS165, Hertz DCX 165.3, JL Audio C2-650. Need a 50-75W RMS amp to make these sing.

The biggest improvement on stock head unit power (~15W/ch) is replacing the dead factory paper drivers with anything modern. Step up to a 4-channel amp (Alpine MRV-F300) for a major leap.

Why it works

  • massive improvement over dead paper drivers
  • manageable install
  • wide price range

Trade-offs

  • pre-97 has only dash 3.5" stock
  • stock head unit limits potential

Tools required

  • trim tool
  • screwdriver
  • wire stripper
  • crimper
