Cooper Discoverer AT3 XLT

Difficulty 1/50 hrs$220–3001984-1990, 1991-1995, 1996, 1997-2001

Cooper's AT3 XLT is a tough, snow-rated all-terrain that pairs nicely with overland XJs.

The AT3 XLT is the heavier-duty version of Cooper's AT3 line — bigger lugs, severe-snow rated, longer treadlife warranty than most. It's quiet for an AT and well-suited to the overland-XJ niche. Common sizes: 31x10.50R15, 33x12.50R15.

Places below the KO2 in the rock-bite ranking but slightly above the Wildpeak. Solid all-rounder.
