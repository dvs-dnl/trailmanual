Wiper Blade Replacement

Difficulty 1/50.1–0.2 hrs$15–401984-1990, 1991-1995, 1996, 1997-2001

Stock wipers are 18 in driver and 18 in passenger. Hook-style attachment. Rear hatch wiper is 12 in with a specific J-hook adapter.

XJ uses two 18-inch front wiper blades (driver and passenger same length) with a standard J-hook arm attachment. Rain-X or Bosch ICON in 18 in are popular upgrades. The rear hatch wiper is a 12-inch blade with a different attachment - many parts stores list it incorrectly; the Trico 12-G or Anco 12-O is the correct rear unit. For best visibility, replace front blades twice a year or whenever they chatter. Wipe the glass with a microfiber and isopropyl alcohol before installing new blades to remove any residual silicone oils that prevent the new rubber from making clean contact.
