Old Man Emu 3" Lift (OME)

Difficulty 3/510–16 hrs$1000–15001984-1990, 1991-1995, 1996, 1997-2001

The ride-quality benchmark for XJ lifts. ARB's Old Man Emu kit uses progressive coils and twin-tube nitro shocks; widely considered the best on-road lift at this height.

Old Man Emu (OME) by ARB is the gold standard for an XJ lift that has to commute every day. The kit pairs progressive-rate front coils, a full replacement rear leaf pack (heavy or medium load options), and OME Nitrocharger Sport twin-tube shocks. Real lift is 2.5"-3" depending on coil choice (Heavy front coils 939 and Heavy rear pack CS013R sit higher; Medium options sit lower).

What sets OME apart is on-road ride. The progressive coils are softer in the first half of travel than RC or Rusty's, and the Nitrocharger shocks valve well for street use. Expect to pay $1000-1400 for the kit depending on shock package. There is no transfer case drop, no track bar relocation, and no extended brake lines included; you'll source those separately.

For overland / daily-driver builds OME is unmatched. For dedicated trail rigs OME is sometimes criticized for being too soft (heavy steel bumpers and a winch will compress the front, and aggressive trail use can blow out the twin-tube shocks). The rear leaf pack is rated for additional load (the Heavy pack handles ~330 lbs constant rear load — bumper, swing-out, fuel cans). Pirate4x4 and Expedition Portal both rate OME XJ as the top "daily/overland" lift at this height.

Why it works

  • Best on-road ride at this height
  • Progressive coils
  • Load-rated rear leaf options
  • Excellent overland choice

Trade-offs

  • Expensive vs. RC
  • Twin-tube shocks fade under hard trail use
  • No bracketry/brake lines included
  • Soft for heavy steel bumpers

Tools required

  • floor jack
  • jack stands
  • metric sockets
  • spring compressor
  • torque wrench
  • leaf spring U-bolt tools
