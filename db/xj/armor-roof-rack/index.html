Roof Rack / Cargo Basket

Difficulty 3/52–6 hrs$200–15001984-1990, 1991-1995, 1996, 1997-2001

Roof racks (Gobi, Hutchinson, Smittybilt) provide cargo carrying for overland builds. Drip-rail mounted XJs need specific brackets; high-roof XJs and Sport models differ.

The XJ's flat roof and tall sides are perfect for a roof rack — significant cargo capacity, RTT support, and gear mounting. Roof rack options:

  • Gobi Stealth: ~$1500. Premium, modular, full-length design, mounted to the rain gutters. Industry standard for overland builds.
  • Hutchinson XJ rack: ~$900. Welded steel, classic look, full-length.
  • Smittybilt Defender rack: ~$600. Budget choice, full-length basket.
  • ARB Base Rack: ~$1200. Modular flat platform for RTT mounting.
  • DIY: $150-300 in steel tubing with weld time.

Mounting points: 1984-1996 XJs have drip rails along the roof; 1997-2001 XJs (with the "flush" gutter design) require track-mounted feet. Verify rack compatibility with your year.

Load capacity: depends on rack + roof — the XJ unibody can take ~150 lbs static and ~75 lbs dynamic. Don't load a roof rack like a flat-bed truck; the high center of gravity affects handling and the unibody mounting points stress over time.

Use cases:

  • Hi-Lift jack and traction boards.
  • Roof-top tent.
  • Lumber/long cargo.
  • Spare fuel cans.
  • Awning mounting.

The "safari rack" full-length style is the iconic XJ look. Combine with rear bumper tire carrier for a complete overland exterior package.

Why it works

  • Massive cargo capacity
  • RTT mounting
  • Gear organization
  • Iconic look

Trade-offs

  • Weight at high CG
  • Wind noise
  • Load capacity limited
  • Cost (premium racks)

Tools required

  • drill (track mounting)
  • metric sockets
  • torque wrench
