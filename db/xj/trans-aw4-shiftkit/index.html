AW4 Governor / Shift Kit

Difficulty 2/51–3 hrs$30–1201987-1990, 1991-1995, 1996, 1997-2001

The AW4 doesn't use a mechanical governor — shift points are TCU-controlled. 'Shift kit' on this trans means valve body modifications (see valvebody entry).

Unlike older 3-speed autos with mechanical governors, the AW4 uses electronic shift control. Shift points are set by the TCU based on vehicle speed (VSS), throttle position (TPS), and shift mode (Power/Comfort).

What 'shift kit' means on an AW4 is valve body modification — see trans-aw4-valvebody for that.

What you can change electronically:

  • Power/Comfort button: stock feature, holds gears longer in Power mode. Use it.
  • TPS adjustment: a worn TPS causes the TCU to misread throttle and shift wrong. Adjust per FSM, replace if voltage sweep is noisy.
  • VSS calibration: bigger tires shift the speed signal. Either change the speedo gear in the t-case to match new tire size, or fit an SGI-5 calibrator that scales the VSS signal in real time. SGI-5 (~$80) restores correct shift points after a tire/gear change.
  • TCU swap: some 96-01 TCUs have slightly different schedules. Mostly noise; the SGI fix is the real one.

If you have 33s+ and never recalibrated, your AW4 is shifting too late and probably annoying you. Recalibrating the VSS often makes the trans 'feel' fixed with no actual rebuild.

  • cheap

Trade-offs
