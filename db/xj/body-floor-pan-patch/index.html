Floor Pan Patch (Driver + Passenger)

Difficulty 3/56–16 hrs$100–4001984-1990, 1991-1995, 1996, 1997-2001

Floor pans rot under carpet from cowl leaks and seam-sealer failure. Patch with 18-gauge steel cut to fit, or buy pre-formed XJ floor patches from KEY Parts / Magnum / Tabco.

XJ floor pans rot in three predictable areas:

1) Driver's footwell, immediately rearward of the firewall, on the right side of the brake/clutch pedal — water from the cowl drain runs here.

2) Passenger footwell, mirror location.

3) Rear seat foot area, under rear cargo.

Pre-formed patches are available:

  • KEY Parts XJ driver/passenger floor patch: ~$80 each, 18-gauge steel, includes flange for spot welding.
  • Tabco / Magnum: similar quality, varies in shape coverage.
  • Auto Rust Technicians: also makes XJ-specific patches.

For smaller rust holes (<6" diameter), 18-gauge sheet steel cut to size with a flange beat into it works fine. Larger areas need the full patch.

Process:

1) Pull carpet (see carpet replacement entry — good time to do both).

2) Cut out rust with cut-off wheel, going 1" beyond visible damage.

3) Treat surrounding metal with phosphoric acid.

4) Fit patch. Mark and cut for tight fit (1/16" gap or less).

5) Tack weld at corners, then stitch weld around perimeter with MIG. Avoid blow-through with short bursts on thin metal.

6) Grind welds smooth. Apply seam sealer over weld inside and underside.

7) Coat underside with rubberized undercoating (3M, Por-15, or Raptor Liner).

8) Inside floor: lay sound deadener (see Dynamat entry) then carpet.

While carpet is out: address the cowl drains (see cowl repair) — they're WHY the floor rotted.

Why it works

  • addresses common rust spot
  • good time to do sound deadening

Trade-offs

  • requires welding
  • must fix cowl drains too

Tools required

  • MIG welder
  • cut-off wheel
  • grinder
  • sheet metal shears
  • trim tool
