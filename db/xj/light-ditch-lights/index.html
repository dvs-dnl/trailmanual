Ditch Lights / A-Pillar Pods

Difficulty 2/52–4 hrs$100–6001984-1990, 1991-1995, 1996, 1997-2001

Small LED pods (Baja Designs Squadron, Rigid D-Series, KC Cyclone) mounted on A-pillar brackets to light the trail edge and corners. Most useful aux lights for actual offroad driving.

Ditch lights point outward at roughly 45 degrees from the front corners of the vehicle, illuminating the trail edges that headlights miss. They're more useful than a 50" bar for slow technical driving because they show you the line.

Mounting: A-pillar brackets bolt to the upper windshield hinge bolts or replace the upper cowl trim screw. XJ-specific brackets are made by JCR Offroad ($90), Hyline Offroad ($80), and various Etsy fabricators. They don't require drilling the body.

Light selection — keep them small (2-3"):

  • Baja Designs Squadron Sport ($170 each) or Pro ($240 each): premium, 3150-4900 lumens. Wide cornering or driving combo beam.
  • Rigid D-Series Pro: similar tier, slightly less output.
  • KC Cyclone V2 ($90 each): mid-tier.
  • Diode Dynamics SS3 Sport ($130 each): excellent value, multiple beam patterns.
  • Auxbeam / generic ($30 each pair): work, but won't last.

Wire as a pair through a single relay, draw is typically 2-5A per pod. Use wide cornering beam pattern, not spot — you want to see SIDES of the trail, not 500 yards down.

Why it works

  • best lights for slow technical driving
  • no body drilling needed
  • useful for backing into camp

Trade-offs

  • catch on tree branches
  • more wiring than a single bar
