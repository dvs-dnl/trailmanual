Dana 30 Chromoly Axle Shafts

Difficulty 4/56–10 hrs$450–15001984-1990, 1991-1995, 1996, 1997-2001

Chromoly shaft upgrade (Yukon, RCV, Ten Factory) increases torsional strength ~50% over stock and adds 1310/1330 U-joints for 33"+ tire reliability.

Stock Dana 30 axle shafts are 27-spline 1023 carbon steel with 297 U-joints. These work fine for stock-to-31" tires but twist or shear under aggressive trail use with 33"+ tires. Upgrading to chromoly shafts is the single biggest reliability gain for a D30 running larger tires.

Option tiers:

  • Yukon Super Joint axles (4340 chromoly, 27-spline outer, 1310 U-joints): ~$450/set, the best value mid-tier upgrade. Available with stock or upgraded U-joints.
  • Ten Factory chromoly axles: 27 or 30-spline, similar quality to Yukon, ~$500/set.
  • RCV Performance CV axles: replaces U-joints with constant-velocity joints (CVs) for maximum power transmission at full steering lock. ~$1500/set. Most extreme upgrade, popular for hardcore rock crawlers.
  • Spicer 1310/1330 super joint shafts: Spicer's own offering, well made, ~$500/set.

30-spline shaft upgrade: some chromoly shaft kits include 30-spline outers, which require a 30-spline locker or carrier. This is the path for a serious D30 build.

Bigger U-joints: 1310 is the next size up from stock 297; 1330 is larger still. Bigger U-joints handle more torque but reduce steering angle slightly because they're physically bigger.

Install: requires pulling the axle shafts (cotter pin, axle nut, brake/rotor, knuckle disassembly). 4-6 hours per side first time. Pair with new ball joints (do them while you're in there).

Why it works

  • ~50% stronger than stock
  • Bigger U-joints for 33s+
  • Reliability gain
  • Many tier options

Trade-offs

  • Expensive
  • Install is involved
  • Bigger U-joints reduce turning radius

Tools required

  • floor jack
  • jack stands
  • metric sockets
  • torque wrench
  • U-joint press
  • ball joint press (recommended)
