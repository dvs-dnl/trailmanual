Dana 30 ARB Air Locker

Difficulty 5/58–14 hrs$850–15001984-1990, 1991-1995, 1996, 1997-2001

ARB Air Locker (RD100 for 3.55-down carrier, RD101 for 3.73-up) is the gold-standard selectable locker for the Dana 30. Engaged on demand via dash switch.

ARB Air Lockers are the premium selectable locker — open differential for normal driving, fully locked (100% torque to both wheels) when engaged via dash switch. The locker is engaged by air pressure (12-150 PSI) from an onboard compressor (typically the ARB CKMA12 or CKMTA12). Engagement is instantaneous and reliable.

Dana 30 ARB Air Lockers:

  • ARB RD100: fits 3.07-3.54 ring gear ratios (the LOW carrier break, where the carrier itself is sized for lower numerical ratios).
  • ARB RD101: fits 3.73 and numerically higher (4.10, 4.56, 4.88, 5.13). This is the more common XJ application.

Pair the locker with: ARB CKMA12 compressor (~$300), wiring kit (~$80), and air line plumbing. Total locker + compressor + install kit is around $1100-1500 plus install labor.

Install: requires pulling the differential, replacing the carrier with the ARB carrier (which has the locker mechanism built in), setting backlash and bearing preload, and drilling/tapping the diff housing for the air line port. Best done by an experienced gear shop unless you've set up differentials before. Total labor 6-10 hours.

Pros: instant on/off, doesn't affect on-road behavior, fully serviceable, ARB customer support is excellent. Cons: requires air supply, more expensive than lunchbox or auto-lockers, longer install.

Why it works

  • Selectable on/off
  • 100% lock when engaged
  • Pairs with onboard air
  • Excellent reliability

Trade-offs

  • Most expensive locker option
  • Requires onboard air
  • Complex install

Tools required

  • differential setup tools (bearing puller, dial indicator, inch-lb torque wrench)
  • drill and tap
  • air line tools
  • wiring tools
