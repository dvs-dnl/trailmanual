Toyo Open Country M/T

Difficulty 1/50 hrs$290–4201984-1990, 1991-1995, 1996, 1997-2001

Toyo's MT is a classic aggressive mud-terrain — heavy, loud, but tough as nails and straightforward to find.

The Toyo Open Country M/T is one of the longest-running MT tires on the market. Big aggressive lugs, very stiff sidewall (3-ply), and a deserved reputation for being nearly indestructible at the cost of weight and noise. Common XJ sizes: 33x12.50R15, 35x12.50R15.

It's a popular choice on rock-crawler builds where the truck doesn't see much highway. Tread wear is poor on pavement (28k–35k typical with mixed use), and the rubber gets hard in cold weather. Not severe-snow rated.

Why it works

  • tough sidewall
  • aggressive mud/rock pattern
  • widely available

Trade-offs

  • heavy
  • loud
  • wears fast
  • not severe-snow
