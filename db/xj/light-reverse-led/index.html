Reverse Light Upgrade (LED, Bumper-Mounted)

Difficulty 2/52–4 hrs$50–3001984-1990, 1991-1995, 1996, 1997-2001

Stock reverse lights are dim. Add an aux LED flood (Rigid SR-Series 6" or KC Cyclone pair) on the rear bumper, wired to the reverse circuit through a relay.

Factory XJ reverse bulbs (1156) put out maybe 400 lumens. Backing into a campsite at night or onto a trailer ramp is awful. The fix: add a bumper-mounted LED flood that triggers off the reverse circuit.

Wiring: pull the reverse light wire (the lighter green / green-white at the rear tail circuit) and use it to trigger a relay. The relay feeds your aux LED from a fused +12V battery feed. Why a relay? The stock reverse circuit is on the BCM/PCM in 97+ models — drawing 10+A directly off it will damage the module. The relay isolates it.

Light choice:

  • Rigid SR-Series 6" flood (~$200): bright, premium
  • Diode Dynamics SS6 Stage Series (~$200): excellent quality
  • KC Cyclone V2 backup kit (~$150): well-priced
  • Generic 4" 27W cube flood pair (~$30): works fine for most

Mount on the rear bumper, hitch, or under the cargo door using existing hardware. Many aftermarket rear bumpers (JCR, C4) have integrated reverse light cutouts.

Why it works

  • huge visibility improvement
  • cheap project
  • manageable install

Trade-offs

  • must use relay to avoid frying BCM

Tools required

  • crimper
  • drill
  • test light
  • wire taps
