Nitto Trail Grappler M/T

Difficulty 1/50 hrs$290–4101984-1990, 1991-1995, 1996, 1997-2001

Nitto Trail Grappler is the "streetable MT" — quieter than most MTs with a much milder ride.

The Trail Grappler is Nitto's hybrid MT — aggressive enough for serious off-road, but tuned to be noticeably quieter and smoother on pavement than a Toyo MT or BFG KM3. Many XJ owners pick this as the "can I daily-drive an MT" tire. Common sizes: 33x12.50R15, 35x12.50R15.

Wear is comparable to other MTs (~30k mi). The sidewall is good but slightly less stiff than the Toyo MT.

Why it works

  • quietest MT in class
  • good on/off road balance

Trade-offs

  • price
  • MT tread wear
  • not severe-snow
