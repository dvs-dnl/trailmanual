LED Headlight Upgrade — 5x7 Sealed-Beam Replacement and Full LED Housings

Difficulty 1/50.5–1.5 hrs$70–6501984-2001

Every Cherokee XJ, 1984 through the final 2001 models, ran the same 5x7-inch H6054 sealed-beam headlight — there was never a composite housing or a replaceable 9004/9007 bulb under the bezel at any point in the run, on any trim. That single spec is why the aftermarket LED options are so interchangeable: every one of them bolts into the same three-screw retaining ring the factory sealed beam used.

Stock halogen sealed beams put out a fraction of what a modern LED delivers, and on a highway-speed platform like the XJ that gap matters more than most owners realize — deer, washouts, and unlit county roads don't wait for your eyes to adjust. The upgrade path splits into two tiers: a direct LED sealed-beam replacement that drops into the stock socket with zero wiring changes, or a full LED housing kit that replaces the entire unit with sealed, DOT-certified optics.

Budget drop-in LED sealed beams (LOYO, Quake LED Tempest, similar 5x7 H6054-spec units) run $70-230/pair and plug straight into the factory socket with no other changes. They're a real upgrade over 40-year-old halogen sealed-beam tech, but the beam pattern quality varies by manufacturer — stick to DOT/SAE-approved units with real reviews, not unbranded listings with stock photos.

The J.W. Speaker 8900 Evolution 2 (~$630-650/pair) is the ceiling: purpose-built LED optics in a sealed IP69K housing, hard-coated anti-fog lens, and a documented beam pattern that meets FMVSS-108. It costs roughly 5x what a budget drop-in costs, but the difference in low-beam cutoff and high-beam throw is not subtle — this is the option trail-driving XJs actually run at night.

Steps

  1. Open the hood and locate the chrome or black trim ring around each headlight — it's held by 2-3 Phillips screws (do not touch the outer adjustment screws at the top and side of the bucket; those set aim, not retention).
  2. Remove the trim ring screws and set the ring aside. Pull the sealed beam forward out of the mounting bucket.
  3. Unplug the factory connector from the back of the sealed beam. Note the connector orientation — the replacement LED unit's plug is keyed the same way.
  4. Plug the new LED headlight into the factory connector. No relay or harness splice is needed for either the LOYO/Quake-style drop-in or the J.W. Speaker housing — both run on the stock circuit.
  5. Seat the new unit in the bucket, reinstall the trim ring, and torque the retaining screws snug — these are small sheet-metal screws, not a torque-spec fastener; snug by hand is correct.
  6. Aim both headlights before driving at night. Park 25 feet from a flat wall, mark the stock beam's high-intensity zone in daylight, then adjust the vertical and horizontal screws so the new LED beam matches that reference. LED beam patterns cut off differently than halogen — skipping this step is the single biggest cause of "my new lights blind everyone."

Why it works

  • Bolts into the stock three-screw bucket and factory connector — no rewiring on either tier
  • Massive light output gain over 1980s-spec halogen sealed beams
  • J.W. Speaker tier is DOT/SAE certified with a real engineered beam pattern, not a repurposed flashlight in a headlight shell

Trade-offs

  • Budget drop-ins vary wildly in beam quality between brands — a bad one throws more glare than light
  • Skipping the aim-and-check step after install is the most common complaint in every XJ forum thread on this swap
  • The J.W. Speaker kit costs more than most owners expect to spend on headlights, even if it's the correct answer for real trail use

Tools required

  • Phillips screwdriver
  • 10mm socket
  • trim panel tool or flathead for the retaining ring
