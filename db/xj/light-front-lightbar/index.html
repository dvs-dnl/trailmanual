Front-Mount Light Bar (40-50 inch) Mounting Options

Difficulty 2/52–5 hrs$100–12001984-1990, 1991-1995, 1996, 1997-2001

Mount a 40-50 inch LED light bar on the front bumper, behind the grille, or on a hidden lower mount. Rigid SR-Series, Baja Designs OnX6, KC Pro6 are top tiers; cheaper Auxbeam/Nilight work fine for casual use.

Mounting locations from least to most invasive:

(1) Lower bumper / behind grille (20-30 inch bar): hidden look, but airflow blocked to radiator if oversized. Works with stock bumper using clamp-on mounts.

(2) Bumper top (40-50 inch bar): most common, fits between hood and bumper. Many aftermarket winch bumpers (Rusty's, Smittybilt XRC, Hanson, JCR) include integrated bar mounts.

(3) Lower windshield/cowl: blocks headlights slightly; not common on XJ since the cowl is fragile.

Quality tiers:

  • Premium ($400-1000): Baja Designs OnX6, KC Pro6, Rigid Industries E/SR series. 5-year warranty, real lumen specs, replaceable optics. Combo beam (spot+flood) is most versatile.
  • Mid ($150-300): Rigid SR-Series, KC Cyclone, Vision X XPR-9M. Good output, decent build.
  • Budget ($40-100): Auxbeam, Nilight, Rough Country. Outputs lie, optics scatter, but works for occasional use.

Wiring: 50" combo bar pulls 15-25A. Run 10 AWG, fuse 25-30A, control via 30A relay. Aim slightly below horizon to avoid blinding oncoming traffic — and use only off-road.

Why it works

  • massive light output for offroad
  • many quality tiers
  • blocks airflow if mounted in grille

Trade-offs

  • not street legal in most states when used
  • cheap bars die fast

Tools required

  • drill
  • step bit
  • crimper
  • socket set
