Viair 400P / 450P portable compressor

Difficulty 1/50 hrs$200–3501984-1990, 1991-1995, 1996, 1997-2001

Viair 400P/450P portables are the budget-friendly tire-fill answer — slower than ARB but a fraction of the cost.

A Viair 400P (or 450P, with longer duty cycle) is a portable compressor that clamps to the battery and fills tires from the engine bay. It's slower than a hardwired twin (will heat-cycle filling four 35s in a row) but costs around $250 and goes anywhere — including borrowed-truck duty. It's the most-recommended starter compressor for the trail.

For faster fills with a portable, MORRflate or AirHead-style 4-tire kits parallel four tires into a manifold so all four fill simultaneously — turns the slow Viair into a reasonable 4-tire airing-up tool.

Why it works

  • affordable
  • portable between vehicles
  • no install required

Trade-offs

  • slower than fixed twins
  • heat cycles on big tires
  • no ARB locker support
