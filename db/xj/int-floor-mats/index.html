Floor Mats - WeatherTech / Husky / Rubber Tubs

Difficulty 1/50.25–1 hrs$30–4001984-1990, 1991-1995, 1996, 1997-2001

Replace the factory carpet floor mats with WeatherTech FloorLiner, Husky X-act Contour, or aftermarket rubber 'tub' mats. Critical for wet/muddy use.

Stock XJ carpet rots from snow, mud, and the leaky cowl. Replacements:

(1) WeatherTech FloorLiner DigitalFit: laser-fit to XJ floorpan, raised edges contain water/mud, channel system directs liquid forward. ~$200-250 for front pair, +$100 for rear. The gold standard. XJ-specific patterns available 1984-2001.

(2) Husky X-act Contour: similar concept to WeatherTech, slightly softer rubber feel. ~$150-200 pair. Equally good for daily driver use.

(3) Husky / Smartliner cargo liner: extends WeatherTech-style protection into cargo area. ~$120.

(4) Generic rubber tub mats (Highland, Custom Accessories): universal-fit cheap option, ~$30 pair. Trim to fit. Works but slides around.

For a true wheeling rig: pull the factory carpet entirely, paint the floor with Rust-Oleum Bedliner or Raptor Liner, install drain plugs (1/2" rubber plugs in factory drain holes are usually still there under carpet), and use the rubber mats as removable surfaces. Hose-out interior.

WeatherTech also makes a Cargo Liner with raised lip and a rear-seat cargo liner for the fold-down position.

Why it works

  • protects carpet from mud/snow
  • straightforward to remove and clean
  • laser-fit

Trade-offs

  • expensive vs generic
  • rear mat is awkward shape on XJ

Tools required

  • none
