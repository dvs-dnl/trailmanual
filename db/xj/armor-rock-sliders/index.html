Rock Sliders / Rocker Armor

Difficulty 4/54–10 hrs$380–6001984-1990, 1991-1995, 1996, 1997-2001

Rock sliders mount to the unibody frame rails to protect the rocker panels — XJ rockers crumple easily, and sliders also act as a high-lift jack point.

The XJ's rocker panels are thin stamped sheet metal welded to the unibody. They crumple on first contact with a rock. Rock sliders bolt or weld to the unibody at the rocker area and provide:

  • Rocker protection (rock impacts hit the slider, not the body).
  • Step / running board functionality.
  • High-Lift jack contact point (drive the jack base under the slider).
  • Additional structural rigidity.

Good options:

  • JCR Offroad XJ sliders: ~$450 (bolt-on, 2" DOM tubing). The most popular XJ slider.
  • Iron Rock Off Road sliders: ~$420.
  • C4 Fabrication rock sliders: ~$550, high-clearance design.
  • Rusty's Off Road sliders: ~$380.
  • DIY: $150 in tubing and weld time.

Mounting styles:

  • Bolt-on through the unibody (requires drilling/sleeves; can leak if not sealed).
  • Weld-on (most rigid, requires welding the unibody — gas tank must be dropped to access).

Note: many "bolt-on" sliders use the rocker pinch seam mounting, which is fine for moderate use but can pull through under hard impact. For serious wheeling, weld-on or hybrid bolt+weld mounting is recommended.

XJ-specific gotcha: the gas tank lives RIGHT next to the driver's-side rocker. Welding sliders requires either dropping the tank or extreme care with heat shields. Many builders drop the tank for slider install.

Why it works

  • Saves the rocker panels
  • High-Lift jack point
  • Step function
  • Adds rigidity

Trade-offs

  • Welding access requires gas tank drop
  • Bolt-on can pull through unibody under hard impact
  • Adds weight

Tools required

  • MIG welder (weld-on)
  • drill (bolt-on)
  • metric sockets
  • torque wrench
