10,000 lb winch — built XJ standard

Difficulty 3/52–4 hrs$600–13001984-1990, 1991-1995, 1996, 1997-2001

10k lb is the sweet spot for a built XJ carrying steel armor, 33-35s, and gear.

Once the truck wears a steel front bumper, rear bumper with tire carrier, swing-out, full skids, and is gear-loaded for a weekend, you can clear 5,000 lb wet weight without trying. A 10k rating gets you back over the 1.5x rule, and the duty cycle handles repeated pulls without throttling. WARN VR EVO 10-S, Smittybilt X2O 10K, and Comeup Seal Gen2 are the popular picks.

Most installs reuse the existing winch plate from an 8k — they share the standard 10" mounting pattern. Confirm your bumper's stated max winch capacity; some budget steel bumpers list a hard 9,500 lb ceiling on the mounting tabs.

Why it works

  • right-sized for typical built XJ
  • longer duty cycle than 8k
  • still light enough for unibody

Trade-offs

  • heavier — adds ~80 lb to the nose
  • needs upgraded charging system for heavy use

Tools required

  • socket set
  • torque wrench
  • wire crimpers
