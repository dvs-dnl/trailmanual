NP242 Selec-Trac Transfer Case Fluid

Difficulty 1/50.5–1 hrs$12–251987-1990, 1991-1995, 1996, 1997-2001

NP242 holds about 1.5 quarts of ATF and is the full-time 4WD option with viscous coupler. Same plugs and procedure as NP231 but watch the slightly higher capacity.

The NP242 was the upmarket Selec-Trac case offering 2WD, Part-Time 4WD, Full-Time 4WD, N, and 4-Low. It contains a viscous coupling that lets you run on dry pavement in Full-Time mode. Capacity is approximately 1.5 quarts of ATF (specifically Mopar ATF+4 per recent service literature; Dexron is the historical fill). Fluid level is checked at the fill plug like the NP231. The NP242 weighs more, runs slightly noisier, and is rarer than the NP231 - many owners hunt them down for daily-driver builds because of the AWD capability. Skip changing fluid only if you know history; many used 242s have neglected fluid that has glazed the viscous coupling. The case is more sensitive to wrong fluid than the NP231 because of the VC - stick to ATF, not gear oil.

Why it works

  • Full-time 4WD for daily driving
  • Same maintenance as NP231

Trade-offs

  • Viscous coupler can be damaged by wrong fluid
  • Heavier

Tools required

  • 8mm hex driver
  • drain pan
  • fluid pump
