Water Pump (Reverse Rotation Note)

Difficulty 2/52–4 hrs$60–1801984-1990, 1991-1995, 1996, 1997-2001

1987-1990 Renix 4.0L uses a reverse-rotation pump (serpentine routed backward). Do not order an HO pump for a Renix — they look identical and will destroy themselves.

The 4.0L water pump is a wear item — bearings whine, shaft develops play, weep hole drips. Symptoms: coolant pinkish stain under the front of the engine, fan wobble, growl at idle. Replace every 100k-150k miles or whenever the symptoms appear.

Critical compatibility note: 1987-1990 Renix XJs use a REVERSE rotation water pump (driven by a serpentine belt routed the opposite direction). The impeller is pitched the opposite way. A standard 1991+ HO pump bolts on identically but will pump backward, leading to overheating and impeller cavitation damage.

When ordering: confirm year and engine. Parts stores routinely list 'fits 1987-2001' which is wrong. Verify by checking the impeller orientation. Gates makes a clearly-marked reverse-rotation pump for Renix (PN 42137). HO uses standard rotation (Gates 41141).

While you're in there: replace the thermostat, hoses, and fan clutch. The labor is mostly the same. Use a quality gasket — the Mopar 53010140 multi-layer composite seals best.
