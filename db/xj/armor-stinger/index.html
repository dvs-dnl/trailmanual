Stinger / Pre-Runner Bumper

Difficulty 2/51–3 hrs$280–7001984-1990, 1991-1995, 1996, 1997-2001

A stinger is a forward-projecting tubular structure that prevents the XJ from rolling forward when nose-down on a steep climb; also doubles as a brushguard.

A stinger is a tubular cage that extends forward from the front bumper and curves upward, designed to catch the vehicle before the windshield contacts the ground in a forward roll on a steep climb. Pre-runner bumpers add a tall hoop or grille guard for desert running and brush protection.

Good options:

  • C4 Fabrication stinger (add-on to their bumpers): ~$300.
  • JCR Offroad pre-runner: ~$280.
  • Custom shop builds (typically $400-700 from a local fab shop).

Stingers are particularly useful for:

  • Rock crawling with steep ascents where roll-forward is a risk.
  • Desert running where brush impact is common.
  • Aesthetic preference (a stinger is an iconic look).

Downsides:

  • Adds significant weight high and forward.
  • Reduces front approach angle if poorly designed.
  • Can block radiator airflow (most are designed around this).
  • Visibility — large stingers can obstruct close-in forward sight.

Most XJ builds skip the stinger unless committing to a hardcore rock crawler. For overland and moderate trail, a sleek high-clearance bumper without stinger is more practical.

Why it works

  • Prevents nose-over rolls
  • Brush protection
  • Iconic look

Trade-offs

  • Adds weight high and forward
  • Can block airflow
  • Reduces approach angle

Tools required

  • metric sockets
  • torque wrench
