Brake / Tail Light LED Upgrade

Difficulty 1/50.5–1.5 hrs$30–1501984-1990, 1991-1995, 1996, 1997-2001

Replace dim 1157 brake bulbs with LED 1157 from Philips, Diode Dynamics, or Sylvania Zevo. Brighter, faster turn-on (~50ms faster than incandescent = bigger safety margin).

Stock 1157 brake bulbs are double-filament incandescent — dim, slow to come up, and short-lived. LED 1157 replacements turn on roughly 50ms faster (a car length at 60 mph), draw less current, and last the life of the truck.

Key considerations:

  • Get a 1157 with the proper offset pins (some clones get this wrong). Bay15d base.
  • Resistor or load-equalizer may be needed for hyperflash (turn signal blinking fast because the BCM detects 'bulb out' from low LED current). On pre-97 XJ no BCM exists — straight relays — so usually no hyperflash. On 97-01 it can happen; add a 6 ohm 25W ceramic resistor across each turn signal LED if it hyperflashes.
  • Brake-only LEDs are most straightforward; the third brake light (CHMSL) is also 1157 on most XJs.

Good options: Diode Dynamics XP80 1157 ($25 each), Philips Ultinon LED 1157 ($20 each), Sylvania ZEVO LED 1157 ($25 each). Whole-truck refresh runs ~$80-120 for tail, brake, CHMSL, and turn signals.

For an extra safety improvement, install a brake-light flasher module (Pulse Modulator) that flashes the brake light 3-4 times briefly when you press the pedal — significantly reduces rear-end collisions.

Why it works

  • faster turn-on = safer
  • less draw
  • longer life

Trade-offs

  • hyperflash on 97-01 sometimes
  • cheap clones fit poorly

Tools required

  • screwdriver
