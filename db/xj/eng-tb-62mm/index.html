62mm vs 56mm Throttle Body

Difficulty 2/51–3 hrs$100–3501984-1990, 1991-1995, 1996, 1997-2001

Quick reference: HO XJ throttle bodies are nominal 60mm; bored 62mm is the common aftermarket. Renix is 52mm — a real bottleneck.

Stock TB sizes by era:

  • Renix (1987-1990): 52mm — measurably restrictive even on stock engine
  • HO (1991-2001): nominal 60mm (measured ~56mm at the smallest point) — adequate on stock 4.0

A bored 62mm or 65mm TB is the common upgrade path. Hesco offers a 62mm bored unit and a 65mm 'Mustang adapter' that uses a Ford 65mm TB on the AMC plenum.

For a stock HO 4.0L: bored TB is worth 1-3 whp at the very top of the rev band. Not worth it as a standalone.

For a Renix 4.0L: an HO TB conversion is well worth doing as part of a broader HO upgrade.

For a stroker: 62mm minimum, 65mm for big builds.

The IAC and TPS bolt patterns are different between Renix and HO. If you swap a HO TB onto a Renix you need to also swap the IAC and TPS. Hesco sells adapter brackets.

Why it works

  • measurable on Renix
  • real on stroker

Trade-offs

  • marginal on stock HO
