Group size matters

This list assumes you might be trailing solo or with a group that doesn't have a support vehicle. In a group of 3+ rigs, you can spread the recovery gear across vehicles — one rig doesn't need to carry it all. The further you are from help, the more self-sufficient each rig should be.

The most important recovery item is a communication plan — satellite communicator or a pre-arranged check-in schedule with someone who knows where you are. Tools and gear don't replace a way to call for help if something goes seriously wrong.
