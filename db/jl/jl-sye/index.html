Jeep Wrangler JL / Drivetrain

SYE Kit — Slip Yoke Eliminator for Lifted JL Wrangler

Difficulty 3/53–6 hrs$320–6502018-2024Jump to steps
Short answer

A 3.5"+ lift on the JL changes the rear driveshaft angle enough that the stock slip yoke — riding on a fixed-length driveshaft — produces vibration at highway speed. The SYE replaces the slip yoke with a fixed output yoke and pairs it with a longer, double-cardan driveshaft that handles the steeper angle cleanly.

The JL Wrangler's rear driveshaft uses a slip yoke at the transfer case output. At stock ride height this works — the driveshaft angle is shallow enough that the slip yoke doesn't bind or produce vibration. Add 3.5" or more of lift and the angle steepens past the point where the slip yoke can handle it. The result is a vibration that appears at 45–70 mph, often described as a rhythmic buzz or drone that changes with speed. It's not subtle once it sets in.

The SYE (Slip Yoke Eliminator) kit replaces the stock slip-yoke output flange with a fixed output flange and internally relocates the slip point. This converts the transfer case output to a fixed yoke, which then mates to a longer, CV-joint driveshaft. The CV joint at the front of the driveshaft cancels the u-joint vibration that causes the problem at steep angles. The result: a JL lifted to 4.5" or more that drives on the highway like a stock truck.

JL Transfer Case Identification

The JL uses different transfer cases depending on trim and model year. Quadratec's 2021 JL specs list the NV241OR Rock-Trac (2-speed, 4:1 low range) on the Rubicon, the NV241 Command-Trac on the Sport, either the NV241 or the full-time MP3022 Selec-Trac on the Sahara, and the MP3022 on the Rubicon 392. Confirm your transfer case before ordering — kits are transfer case-specific, and a kit built for the NV241OR will not fit the MP3022. Pull the skid plate and read the transfer case ID tag if you aren't sure.

Kit + Driveshaft — Buy Together

Order the SYE kit and the replacement rear driveshaft together, from the same vendor if possible. The driveshaft length must be measured for your specific lift height and suspension type — most vendors require you to specify lift height and whether you have a long-arm or short-arm suspension at the time of order. A mismeasured driveshaft is the most common reason SYE installs produce vibration post-install.

Tom Woods is the premier shop for this application. Their kits are built to order, the driveshaft comes indexed for correct phasing, and their support for JL-specific nuances is established. Advance Adapters makes a quality kit for the NV241OR specifically. Rough Country's bundled option is the budget path and works for moderate-lift builds.

Steps

  1. Drain the transfer case fluid. Support the skid plate and remove it.
  2. Measure and photograph the existing driveshaft angle before removal.
  3. Remove the rear driveshaft at both ends. Mark the orientation at the pinion flange for reinstallation reference.
  4. Support the transfer case with a transmission jack. Remove the crossmember if required per the SYE instructions.
  5. Remove the stock slip yoke from the transfer case output shaft per the kit instructions — typically requires a slide hammer or yoke puller.
  6. Install the SYE internal components per the kit's sequence: output bearing, new seal, fixed output flange.
  7. Reinstall the transfer case crossmember. Torque to factory spec.
  8. Install the new double-cardan CV driveshaft. Verify phasing marks align (u-joint ears in phase).
  9. Torque driveshaft flange bolts to 18–22 ft-lb at the pinion end and 30 ft-lb at the transfer case output.
  10. Refill the transfer case with the manufacturer-specified fluid (typically ATF+4 for JL applications — verify).
  11. Road test at highway speed. Any remaining vibration usually indicates driveshaft phasing or a lingering pinion angle issue — measure pinion angle at ride height and correct with adjustable upper control arms if needed.

Why it works

  • Eliminates the root cause of highway vibration on lifted JLs — not a bandaid fix
  • Fixed output yoke and CV driveshaft handles 4.5"–6"+ of lift cleanly
  • Improves rear driveshaft life by running correct angles instead of fighting the geometry
  • Tom Woods and Advance Adapters have decades of Jeep drivetrain experience behind their kits

Trade-offs

  • Requires transfer case-specific parts — must confirm your TC model before ordering
  • Driveshaft must be measured for your lift height — not an off-the-shelf part
  • More involved than bolt-on suspension work — transfer case output work requires care with seals and bearing installation
  • If pinion angle is also wrong (common after a lift), the SYE alone won't cure all vibration — both need attention

Tools required

  • socket set
  • torque wrench
  • transmission jack
  • floor jack
  • pry bar
  • drain pan
  • snap ring pliers
  • yoke puller or slide hammer
