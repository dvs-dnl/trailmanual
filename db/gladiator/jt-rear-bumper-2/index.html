Rear Bumper for the Gladiator JT — Pickup Bed Integration

Difficulty 3/52–4 hrs$799–15992020-2024

The JT's rear bumper is completely different from any Wrangler rear — the pickup bed floor and tailgate change every mounting dimension, and a Wrangler rear bumper will not fit.

This is the point where Gladiator owners most often get burned by shopping the wrong vehicle. The JL Wrangler's rear bumper is a straightforward crossmember-and-tube unit that clears the cargo area. The JT's rear bumper has to account for the pickup bed floor sitting several inches lower than a Wrangler's tub, the tailgate's lower pivot point, and the bed's frame rails extending rearward. JT-specific rear bumpers are built around those dimensions — they're a different part designed from scratch. If a listing doesn't say JT, it won't fit.

Beyond fitment, the JT's rear also involves two practical questions most buyers don't think about until they're partway through the install: hitch receiver placement and backup camera retention. Most JT owners want to keep the hitch receiver for towing — the JT is rated to tow up to 7,650 lbs depending on powertrain — and not all aftermarket rear bumpers include a receiver tube or position it at the same height as the factory unit. Verify hitch integration before ordering. Backup camera retention is similarly variable: some bumpers include a camera relocation bracket; others require you to delete the camera or source an aftermarket mount.

The Warn Ascent JT rear ($1,399) handles both issues cleanly — it includes a full-size 2" receiver and a camera port in the correct location. Addictive Desert Designs' JT rear ($1,299) is a more aggressive-looking steel unit popular on trail-focused builds, with shackle mounts and recovery points rated for recovery loads. Poison Spyder's JT Crusher ($1,199) is the budget leader in the heavy-steel category, with a functional if less refined finish.

Why it works

  • Replaces the factory plastic lower valance with steel protection at the most vulnerable departure-angle impact point
  • JT-specific designs maintain hitch receiver position and backup camera function
  • Recovery-point integration provides rated anchor points not present on the factory bumper
  • Improves departure angle on lifted JTs by pulling the lower bumper edge up and in

Trade-offs

  • No JL/Wrangler crossover — this is a JT-only part; selection is narrower than the front bumper market
  • High price tier relative to rear bumpers on most other platforms
  • Backup camera relocation or deletion required on most installs
  • Heavy steel adds weight directly behind the rear axle — factor into towing tongue weight calculations

Tools required

  • socket set
  • torque wrench
  • pry bar
  • electrical connectors
  • floor jack for weight support
