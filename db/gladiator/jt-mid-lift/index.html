Mid-Tier Lift for the Gladiator — 2.5"-3.5" with Geometry Correction ($700–$2,000)

Difficulty 3/56–10 hrs$699–20002020-2024

The 2.5"–3.5" range is where the Gladiator JT's suspension setup pays off — enough lift for 35s on any trim, enough height to address departure angle on the truck bed, and the geometry correction that separates a good lift from a frustrating one.

At this price tier you're buying replacement coils up front, not spacers, plus control arm corrections and track bar adjustments that keep the front axle centered and the handling predictable on-road. The Teraflex 2.5" Performance kit ($699) leads the value conversation — it's replacement coils with alignment cam bolts and an adjustable front track bar, which addresses most of the geometry shortcomings of a 2.5" lift without going to full adjustable control arms. The JKS 2.5" kit ($899) adds better shocks and a slightly more complete geometry package. EVO Mfg's Enforcer at $1,299 is the proper build: front and rear addressed together, quality control arms, and EVO's reputation for JL/JT fitment accuracy.

The Mopar 2" kit ($995) deserves a mention specifically for owners who want to preserve their factory powertrain warranty. It's dealer-installed, FIAT/Chrysler-approved, and Mopar will stand behind it if anything goes sideways with warranty claims. It won't give you the wheel travel or geometry correction of the aftermarket kits, but the warranty peace-of-mind is real for newer trucks still under coverage.

The JT's rear leaf spring suspension is the ongoing compatibility headache with this platform. Every JL-based lift kit you find on the shelf was designed around the JL's rear coil springs. Some manufacturers sell JT-specific versions of their JL kits; others retrofit the front components and include JT-specific rear leaf spring packs or AAL kits separately. Before you order anything, confirm the listing says JT — not JL — and verify it includes a rear component. Running a leveled front with stock rear leaves on a truck that hauls gear will look wrong and handle worse than stock.

Why it works

  • Clears 35" tires on all JT trims with no rubbing
  • Geometry correction included — adjustable track bar and cam bolts prevent handling problems
  • Full kit approach addresses both front coil and rear leaf spring in one purchase
  • Mopar option preserves factory warranty coverage

Trade-offs

  • Rear leaf spring compatibility requires careful verification before ordering — JL kits will not work
  • Higher cost and complexity vs. budget spacer kits
  • Professional alignment required after install ($80–$150)
  • Aftermarket shocks in mid-tier kits are adequate but not exceptional — shock upgrade is a common follow-on purchase
