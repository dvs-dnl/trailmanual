Oil Change — Jeep Gladiator JT 3.6L Pentastar V6

Difficulty 1/50.5–1 hrs$35–752020-2024

The Gladiator's 3.6L Pentastar V6 requires full synthetic oil — 0W-20 is the factory spec. Using the correct viscosity matters more than the brand; using conventional oil in this engine is a mistake.

The 3.6L Pentastar powers every JT Gladiator. It's a robust, well-proven engine with one known sensitivity: it requires full synthetic oil at all times. The tight tolerances in the Pentastar's variable valve timing (VVT) system are designed around synthetic oil's viscosity characteristics — conventional oil doesn't flow fast enough at cold start to properly lubricate the VVT actuators, and degraded synthetic causes sludging in the VVT passages more readily than the spec'd fluid.

Jeep's oil life monitor (OLM) uses engine parameters (temperature cycles, idling time, load) to estimate when the oil should be changed. For most drivers, this works out to roughly 7,000–10,000 miles between changes. For a Gladiator doing heavy trail work with sustained low-speed high-load operation, pull the interval in to 5,000–7,500 miles — the OLM doesn't fully account for trail-use abuse.

**Oil capacity:** 5.9 quarts with filter change. Have 6 quarts on hand — drain capacity varies slightly by how long the oil drains.

**Oil filter:** The Pentastar uses a cartridge-style oil filter (not a spin-on canister). The housing is on the front of the engine, passenger side, accessible from above with a 13/16" socket or the Mopar filter cap tool (PN: 4884899AC). Mopar's OEM filter fits correctly; WIX 57356, Fram Ultra XG10575, and Purolator Boss PBL14621 are compatible aftermarket alternatives.

  • **Overtightened drain plug**: Torque to 20 ft-lbs — that's hand-tight plus about 1/4 turn. Overtightening strips the aluminum pan threads. If the threads feel suspect, use a thread repair kit (Timesert, not Helicoil) before the next change.
  • **Filter housing O-ring**: If you forget to replace the O-ring and the old one is compromised, the housing will weep oil. This is the most common cause of post-oil-change leaks on the Pentastar.
  • **Underfill vs. overfill**: Overfilling by more than 1 quart can cause the crankshaft to whip oil into foam, reducing lubrication. The JT's 5.9-qt capacity is precise — add, check the stick, add again in small amounts.

Mobil 1 0W-20 (5 qt jug): ~$30–35. Industry-standard quality at a reasonable price.

Mopar OEM filter: ~$12–16 at the dealer or through online Mopar parts suppliers. The correct specification, not worth compromising on.

Full DIY kit (6 qt oil + OEM filter + new drain plug washer): ~$50–55. A dealer oil change on a JT typically runs $75–100 depending on location. DIY saves $25–50 per interval.

Tools required

  • oil filter wrench
  • drain pan
  • funnel
  • rags
  • 13/16" socket or oil filter cap (PN: 4884899AC)
