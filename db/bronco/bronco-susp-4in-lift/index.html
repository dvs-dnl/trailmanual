4" Suspension Lift — Trail Build Baseline

Difficulty 3/58–20 hrs$800–22001966-1977

4" is the standard trail build lift for an early Bronco. It fits 35" tires with proper offset wheels, gives real articulation improvement, and stays within a range where you can run good shocks without reservoir canisters hanging everywhere.

A 4" suspension lift is the baseline for a capable early Bronco trail build. It provides:

  • 35" tire clearance (requires 3.75"–4.5" backspacing wheels)
  • Improved departure and approach angles
  • Real articulation benefit over a 2" lift
  • Extended driveshaft angles that are manageable without SYE

**Key components:**

  • **Springs:** Superlift, Old Man Emu (OME), and Skyjacker all make early Bronco-specific kits. Skyjacker's 4" kit is the most popular at ~$950–1100 complete.
  • **Shocks:** 4" of lift requires extended-travel shocks. Bilstein 5100 series or Fox 2.0 IFP shocks work well. Do not run stock-length shocks — they will top out and damage bumpstops.
  • **Brake lines:** Stock brake lines will go taut at 4" of lift. Stainless braided extended lines ($80) are required for safety.
  • **Sway bar links:** Stock links are too short at 4". Extended links ($60) or disconnect units allow the suspension to flex on trail.
  • **Geometry:** Caster drops ~2° at 4" of lift. A 2° caster correction kit or an alignment shop with caster adjustment can correct this.

**Driveshaft:** At 4", front driveshaft angles increase but stay within U-joint operating range. A rear SYE (slip yoke eliminator) is not required at 4" with stock suspension travel — reconsider if adding a locker and expecting aggressive articulation.

Why it works

  • fits 35" tires
  • real articulation and departure angle improvement
  • complete kits available
  • bolt-on with no fabrication (except brake lines)

Trade-offs

  • extended brake lines required (safety-critical)
  • geometry correction needed
  • front driveshaft angles increase — inspect annually
  • cost is 3-4x a 2" kit

Tools required

  • floor jack
  • jack stands
  • spring compressor
  • torque wrench
  • angle grinder
  • welder (if extended brake lines)
