Trail Build Recipe — 35s, Dana 44 Lockers, 4" Lift

Difficulty 4/580–180 hrs$15000–350001966-1977

This is the standard capable trail build for an early Bronco — not a show truck, not a dedicated rock crawler, but a truck that will handle moderate-to-difficult trails reliably and still drive on the street.

**Starting point:** 1969–1977 Bronco with solid floors and frame. Rust-compromised structure adds $5K–$15K to this budget before the build begins.

**Engine and drivetrain:**

  • 5.0L HO engine swap (if starting from an I6 or tired 302): ~$2,200 total with Edelbrock intake and carb
  • Shorty headers + 2.5" dual exhaust: ~$650
  • Pertronix ignition: $90

**Suspension:**

  • Skyjacker 4" complete kit: ~$1,100 (springs, extended shocks, hardware)
  • Extended brake lines: $80 (safety-critical, don't skip)

**Axles and gearing:**

  • ARB Air Lockers front and rear: ~$1,160 for both, plus $340 for the twin compressor and ~$200 for line and mounting
  • Yukon 4.56 gear sets: ~$680 for both axles; add $700–$900 for professional installation

**Tires and wheels:**

  • 35x12.50R15 BFG KO2: ~$1,200 mounted and balanced (5 tires)
  • 15×8 steel wheels, 4.5" backspacing: ~$500 for 5

**Skid plates and armor:**

  • Transfer case skid, engine skid: ~$500 fabricated or purchased
  • Rock sliders (tube frame): ~$400–600 fabricated

**Total range:** $15,000–$35,000 depending on starting condition, DIY vs. professional labor, and shop rate.

**Build order:** engine first (so you're not working around a tired powerplant), then suspension + brake lines + alignment, then axle regear, then lockers. Tires last — confirm clearance before buying.

Why it works

  • capable on moderate-to-difficult trails
  • streetable daily (mostly)
  • ARB lockers are transparent until you need them
  • strong resale as a "clean driver build"

Trade-offs

  • starting condition dramatically affects budget
  • ARB locker installation requires professional axle setup
  • 35s at 4.56 gears with a Dana 20 is marginal — 4.88 preferred for 35s

Tools required

  • floor jack
  • jack stands
  • socket set
  • torque wrench
  • welder (for skids/sliders)
