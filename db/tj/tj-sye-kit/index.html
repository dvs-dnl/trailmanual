SYE Kit for the NP231 Transfer Case — Eliminating Driveshaft Vibration

Difficulty 4/53–5 hrs$299–5991997-2006

Above 2.5" of lift, the NP231's slip-yoke rear output causes driveshaft vibration at highway speed that makes the TJ unpleasant to drive — an SYE kit and CV rear driveshaft eliminates it.

The NP231 transfer case uses a slip-yoke output at the rear — a design where the yoke slides in and out of the output shaft as the driveshaft plunges and extends with suspension travel. At stock ride height, this works fine. Lift the vehicle 2.5"–4" and the rear driveshaft's operating angle steepens beyond what the slip yoke and standard U-joint can absorb without vibration. At highway speed, the resulting vibration feels like a tire imbalance or a failing u-joint — a buzz or thump that comes on around 45–55 mph and worsens as speed increases.

The SYE (Slip Yoke Eliminator) kit replaces the slip yoke output with a fixed-yoke flange. This requires fitting a new rear output shaft with a fixed-yoke companion flange, which then accepts a custom-length CV (constant velocity) rear driveshaft. The CV driveshaft uses a double-cardan joint at the transfer case end that allows steeper operating angles without the velocity variation that causes vibration with a single U-joint. The result is a smooth, vibration-free rear driveshaft at any legal highway speed regardless of lift height.

Tom Wood's SYE kit is the benchmark. Tom Wood's manufactures both the SYE hardware and the companion CV driveshaft, so the entire system is matched and tested. They build custom driveshafts to measured length after the SYE is installed — send them your measurements and they build the shaft to spec. Advance Adapters' SYE ($279) is a reliable, lower-cost alternative that uses the same design principle and works with any quality rear CV shaft.

The Rubicon's NP242 (Select-Trac) transfer case is a different unit with a different output configuration. This SYE kit does not fit the NP242. Rubicon owners with the NP241OR have their own SYE solutions, but the NP241OR is less commonly used outside Rubicon trim and the aftermarket solutions are more limited.

Why it works

  • Completely eliminates highway-speed driveshaft vibration that is otherwise unavoidable above 2.5" of lift
  • Fixed-yoke output is stronger and more reliable than the OEM slip yoke under trail torque loads
  • Tom Wood's matched SYE + CV driveshaft is a proven system with years of TJ trail history
  • Bolt-in install on the NP231 requires no special machining or permanent modifications to the transfer case housing
  • CV driveshaft can accommodate additional lift in the future without needing replacement (within the joint's angle limits)

Trade-offs

  • Does not fit the NP242 (Sahara Select-Trac) — verify your transfer case before purchasing
  • Custom rear driveshaft must be ordered after SYE install and measured correctly — plan for lead time on the driveshaft
  • Transfer case must be fully drained and refilled — use ATF+4, not gear oil
  • Total system cost (SYE + CV driveshaft) runs $550–$650 when both are purchased together — budget accordingly when planning a mid-lift or long-arm install
  • Incorrect pinion angle after installation will cause a different type of vibration — verify angle with a digital angle finder before closing out the install

  • socket set

  • snap ring pliers
  • bearing press
