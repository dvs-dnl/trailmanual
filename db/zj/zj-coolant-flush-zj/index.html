Coolant Flush — 4.0 and Magnum V8

Difficulty 2/51–2 hrs$35–701993-1998

The ZJ 4.0L cooling system holds about 12.7 quarts; the Magnum V8s hold about 14 quarts. Plan on flushing with distilled water twice, then refilling with a 50/50 mix of distilled water and extended-life antifreeze. Bleed the air through the bleed plug on the thermostat housing — skip that step and you'll overheat within 10 miles.

A ZJ that's never had a coolant flush will run hot in Phoenix summer, especially with the AC on, and the heater core will weep onto the passenger floor mat before it finally gives up. The 4.0L straight-six and the Magnum V8s both share an aluminum radiator on a plastic tank — these tanks crack with age, and old acidic coolant accelerates the failure. Flushing every 50,000 miles is the cheapest insurance you can buy against a $250 radiator.

The original Mopar HOAT (red) coolant was good for 5 years or 100,000 miles. Modern Mopar extended-life OAT coolant is fully backward-compatible with the ZJ if the system is fully flushed first. Don't mix coolant chemistries — flush twice with distilled water until what comes out is clear, then refill with a single coolant type. Prestone Cor-Guard, Peak Long Life, and Mopar's current OAT spec all work.

The radiator drain is a plastic petcock on the passenger-side bottom of the radiator. Most are old, brittle, and snap off when you turn them with pliers. Have a $25 replacement petcock on hand before you start. The block drain is a 1/4" NPT plug on the driver's side of the block — pull that too if you want a full flush, but it's optional and clears most of the residual coolant.

Why it works

  • Cheap maintenance — $25 in supplies, no special tools
  • The single biggest predictor of long-term cooling system reliability
  • Catches a failing radiator or weeping water pump before they leave you stuck

Trade-offs

  • The plastic radiator petcock breaks more often than not on a ZJ that's never been serviced — have a spare on hand
  • Air pockets in the heater core will cause overheating even after a "good" flush — bleed the system properly or repeat the bleed procedure after the first drive
  • The old MEG (green) coolant must be fully flushed before refilling with OAT — mixing causes gelling and ruins the water pump

Tools required

  • drain pan
  • garden hose
  • funnel
  • pliers (for the radiator drain petcock)
  • 5-gallon bucket for waste coolant
