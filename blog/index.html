XJ Thermostat Replacement: 195°F or 180°F, and How to Do It Right

Run the 195°F thermostat — it’s the factory spec for the 4.0 and 2.5, and a 180 fixes nothing. A thermostat sets the minimum operating temperature, not the maximum: once coolant is past the rating it’s fully open, and the radiator, fan clutch, and water pump own the outcome. The part runs $8–$20, the job is two bolts and about an hour, and the housing torques to 15 ft-lb (20 N·m) — overtightening those bolts is the most common reason a housing leaks afterward.
