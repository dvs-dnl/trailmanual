Recall History · Wrangler 4XE

4XE Recall History

The Wrangler 4XE has accumulated more recalls in four years of production than most vehicles see in a decade. Some are nuisances. One is a fire risk. Know the difference before you buy.

Wrangler 4xe·2021–2025·Checked October 29, 2026

Start here: run the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls before any other evaluation step. Seller confirmation is not sufficient — verify independently. The campaigns below are current as of October 29, 2026, from the NHTSA filings linked on each card. The VIN lookup decides whether a specific Jeep is covered, and it shows open recalls only, so get the dealer service history for completed work.

Major Recalls

Fire risk
NHTSA 26V363 · FCA 21D

Power-steering pump connector can overheat and catch fire, even with the ignition off

Vehicles: 2021–2025 Wrangler, 4xe included, built June 24, 2020 – Dec 2, 2024 (787,887); also 2021–2025 Gladiator built Aug 18, 2020 – Dec 2, 2024

Owner instruction: Park away from structures and other vehicles until repaired.

Final remedy available, as of 2026-10-29. Inspect; replace the pump wiring and pump as needed. Available since July 9, 2026.

Verify: Check the VIN, and the door-jamb build date against the June 24, 2020 – Dec 2, 2024 window. Warning signs: lost steering assist or a "Service Power Steering" message.

Source: NHTSA, NHTSA

Fire risk
NHTSA 25V741 · FCA 68C

4xe high-voltage battery cells can fail internally and catch fire, parked or driving

Vehicles: 2020–2025 Wrangler 4xe built July 1, 2020 – Aug 25, 2025 (228,221 Wranglers; 320,068 including Grand Cherokee 4xe)

Owner instruction: Don't recharge, and park away from structures and other vehicles, until repaired.

Final remedy available, as of 2026-10-29. Battery analysis and control-module software; the pack is replaced only if trouble codes call for it, with an unlimited-years/miles warranty extension for the defect codes. Vehicles fixed under 95B or B9A still need 68C (nine fires were reported after the 95B fix). Available since Dec 16, 2025.

Verify: Get proof of the 68C remedy itself, not 95B or B9A, and confirm the battery warranty extension applies to this VIN.

Source: NHTSA, NHTSA, NHTSA

Loss of power
NHTSA 25V766 · FCA 78C

Casting sand in the 2.0L engine can cause bearing failure, loss of drive power or an engine fire (4xe)

Vehicles: 2024–2025 Wrangler 4xe built June 7, 2023 – May 30, 2025 (77,928 Wranglers)

Owner instruction: No parking instruction; a knocking noise or check-engine light can come before failure.

Final remedy available, as of 2026-10-29. Engine computer reflash that detects a pending bearing failure; engine or long block replaced if confirmed, with an extended warranty for the defect. Available for 2024–2025 Wranglers since April 2026. Related: 26E021 covers 2,689 suspect Mopar service engines.

Verify: Get proof of the 78C engine software (and any engine replacement) on 2024–2025 builds; listen for knocking on a cold start.

Source: NHTSA, NHTSA

Loss of power
NHTSA 25V710 · FCA A7C

An over-the-air telematics update can make the hybrid control processor reset and cut drive power (4xe)

Vehicles: 2023–2025 Wrangler 4xe built Feb 21, 2023 – June 5, 2025 that received the update (24,238)

Interim action only; final remedy pending, as of 2026-10-29. Interim server-side fix applied to every affected vehicle; the final telematics software update was still being designed as of Stellantis's July 20, 2026 letter.

Verify: Only an interim fix exists. Ask a dealer whether the final telematics update has been released and applied, and ask the seller about any sudden loss of power.

Source: NHTSA, NHTSA

Loss of power
NHTSA 22V865 · FCA ZB7

Lost communication between transmission and hybrid controllers can shut the engine down (4xe)

Vehicles: 2021–2023 Wrangler 4xe built Sep 2, 2020 – Aug 17, 2022 (62,909)

Final remedy available, as of 2026-10-29. Software changes the failsafe from engine shutdown to limp-home; the filing lists the root cause as unknown.

Verify: Confirm the 22V865 software on 2021–2023 builds, and ask whether the Jeep has ever shut down or gone into limp mode while driving.

Source: NHTSA

Open investigation
NHTSA RQ24-012

NHTSA investigation into loss of power on the Wrangler 4xe, including vehicles after the 22V865 fix

Vehicles: 2021–2024 Wrangler 4xe (94,275 estimated)

Open, as of 2026-10-29. NHTSA is assessing whether 22V865's scope and remedy are adequate. No outcome yet.

Verify: No action for an owner yet. Watch for a new recall if NHTSA finds the 22V865 fix inadequate.

Source: NHTSA, NHTSA

Visibility
NHTSA 24V111

Hybrid control software can stop the defroster and defogger from working (4xe)

Vehicles: 2021–2024 Wrangler 4xe (population not re-verified)

Remedy status not confirmed (not re-verified), as of 2026-10-29. Hybrid control processor software update.

Verify: Run the defroster before you buy and confirm the windshield starts to clear.

Source: Trail Manual

What's still open

As of October 29, 2026, the battery-fire (68C) and engine (78C) recalls have remedies available for Wranglers. The over-the-air power-loss recall (A7C) has only an interim fix, and NHTSA's loss-of-power investigation (RQ24-012) is still open. Stellantis said in January 2026 it would phase out plug-in hybrids starting with the 2026 model year, so 2025 is the last Wrangler 4xe. Check nhtsa.gov/recalls for anything filed after this date.

How to Verify Recall Completion

  • 1
    Go to nhtsa.gov/recalls The NHTSA website has a VIN lookup tool that shows any open (unrepaired) safety recalls on a specific vehicle. It does not list recalls that have already been repaired.
  • 2
    Enter the full 17-digit VIN Get the VIN from the driver's door jamb sticker or the dashboard (visible through the windshield). Confirm it matches the title and registration.
  • 3
    Review any open recalls Each open recall is listed by campaign number; open means the remedy has not been recorded as done for that VIN. A clean result doesn't show which recalls were repaired, so get the dealer service history for completed recall work.
  • 4
    For the fire recall: confirm 68C, not just 95B A seller's records may show 95B (or the earlier B9A) as done — that is not sufficient. Nine fires were reported after the 95B fix, and 68C replaced it. Vehicles fixed under 95B or B9A still need 68C. Ask a Stellantis dealer to confirm the current remedy status via their service system before purchase.
  • 5
    Request written recall completion documentation Ask the seller for physical dealer service records showing recall completion. If they don't have them, the dealer can print a service history. Keep a copy in the vehicle's paperwork after purchase.
