Recall History · Wrangler 4XE

4XE Recall History

The Wrangler 4XE has accumulated more recalls in four years of production than most vehicles see in a decade. Some are nuisances. One is a fire risk. Know the difference before you buy.

JL Generation·2021–Present·Verify at nhtsa.gov/recalls

Start here: run the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls before any other evaluation step. Seller confirmation is not sufficient — verify independently. The recall list below is current as of mid-2026; new recalls may have been issued since. The NHTSA VIN lookup is the authoritative source.

Major Recalls

Fire Risk
Recall 95B + Superseding Remedy

HV Battery Fire Risk

Vehicles: 2021–2025, 320,000+ affected

HV battery cells with internal separator damage can experience thermal runaway — a self-sustaining heat reaction that leads to fire while driving or parked and charging. The original software fix (Recall 95B) was later confirmed ineffective in certain cases. Nine fires were reported in vehicles that had already received the 95B fix.

A superseding remedy was subsequently developed. Owners were advised to park outside and stop overnight charging in enclosed spaces until the latest fix was applied.

Verify: Not just that 95B was completed — confirm the LATEST remedy is in the service record. Ask a Stellantis dealer to confirm via VIN lookup. Do not proceed without this verification.
Safety
Recall 22V-865

Loss of Motive Power

Vehicles: 2021–2024, ~94,000 affected

Vehicles shut off without warning while driving. NHTSA opened a formal investigation. Multiple root causes have been identified across the affected population. Complaints were filed for vehicles that had already received the initial fix, indicating incomplete resolution across the fleet.

Verify: Confirm recall completion via NHTSA lookup. Ask the seller directly whether the vehicle has ever shut off unexpectedly while driving.
Safety
Issued February 2024

Windshield Defroster Failure

Vehicles: 2021–2024, ~139,000 affected

The defrost and defog system fails to activate under certain operating conditions, impairing visibility in weather and off-road conditions. A distinct safety concern from the fire recall but often grouped together during dealer visits.

Verify: Confirm recall completion via NHTSA lookup. Test the defroster yourself before purchase — turn it on and confirm the windshield begins to clear.
Software
OTA Update Recall

Flawed OTA Software Update

Vehicles: 2023–2025, ~24,000 affected

A Stellantis over-the-air software update bricked the hybrid control system, causing sudden loss of propulsion. The vehicle required dealer reprogramming to restore normal function. This is a distinct event from Recall 22V-865 — different root cause, different affected population.

Verify: Run VIN through NHTSA to confirm if affected. Dealer can confirm reprogramming status via VIN. Any 2023–2025 example should be checked.
Note on Recall Coverage

This guide covers major recalls current as of mid-2026. The 4XE has an active regulatory history — additional recalls may have been issued after this guide was written. The NHTSA VIN lookup at nhtsa.gov/recalls is the authoritative source and should always be consulted directly.

How to Verify Recall Completion

  • 1
    Go to nhtsa.gov/recalls The NHTSA website has a VIN lookup tool that shows all recalls associated with a specific vehicle, including completion status.
  • 2
    Enter the full 17-digit VIN Get the VIN from the driver's door jamb sticker or the dashboard (visible through the windshield). Confirm it matches the title and registration.
  • 3
    Review all open recalls — note which are incomplete The report shows each recall by number and whether it has been completed for that specific VIN. "Incomplete" means the fix has not been applied.
  • 4
    For the fire recall: confirm the LATEST remedy, not just 95B The NHTSA lookup may show 95B as complete — that is not sufficient. The original 95B software fix was later found ineffective. The superseding remedy is what matters. Ask a Stellantis dealer to confirm the current remedy status via their service system before purchase.
  • 5
    Request written recall completion documentation Ask the seller for physical dealer service records showing recall completion. If they don't have them, the dealer can print a service history. Keep a copy in the vehicle's paperwork after purchase.
