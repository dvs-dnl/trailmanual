Jeep Wrangler JL · 2018–present

JL Trail Spare Parts Guide

The JL is a modern, reliable platform. Its spares list is shorter than the JK’s. Focus on electronics, transfer case fuses, and powertrain-specific items.

Universal Spares — All Vehicles

These items belong in every rig regardless of year or platform. The JL-specific failures follow below.

Driveline
  • U-joint set — matched to your driveshaft (front and rear)
  • Slip yoke eliminator hardware — if you’ve lifted more than 3"
  • Differential cover gasket — carry one per axle
Cooling
  • Thermostat + gasket
  • Upper and lower radiator hoses
  • Coolant (premixed) — 1 gallon minimum
  • Radiator stop-leak — field emergency only, not a repair
Belts & Hoses
  • Serpentine belt — exact match to your year
  • Heater hose sections — 3/4" and 5/8" ID, 12" lengths
Electrical
  • Full fuse assortment — blade type, 5A through 40A
  • Spare relay set — 30A and 40A, SPDT
  • Crank and cam sensor — if your rig is over 100k miles
Fluids
  • Engine oil — 1 quart, matched to your viscosity
  • Gear oil 80W-90 — 1 quart for axle emergencies
  • Brake fluid DOT 3 — 8 oz
  • Power steering fluid — if equipped
Fasteners
  • Assorted M8, M10, M12 bolts and nuts
  • Grade 8 hardware assortment — 3/8" and 1/2"
  • Cotter pins, various sizes

JL-Specific Spares

The JL’s spares list is lean by design — this platform has fewer mechanical failure points than the JK. What it does have is more electronics. Pack accordingly.

Sky One-Touch roof motor brush kit (if equipped) The Sky One-Touch roof motor brushes wear and the roof stops mid-travel. The brush kit is the field fix.
Intercooler hose clamp set (2.0L eTorque) The lower intercooler hose loosens under heat cycling. A properly sized hose clamp tightened one size up prevents the blow-off. Carry two extra clamps as insurance.
Transfer case motor fuse The Selec-Trac and Rock-Trac are electronically controlled. The shift motor fuse blows occasionally. Check your fuse panel diagram and carry the right one. The motor itself rarely fails.
Timing chain tensioner (3.6L, if ticking cold) Same awareness as the JK Pentastar. If yours ticks on cold starts, carry the updated tensioner part number.
Front axle shaft (if running 37"+ on non-Rubicon) JL axle quality is improved over the JK. If you’re running 37"+ tires on a non-Rubicon Dana 30, carry a front shaft as a hedge.
Correct fluids for your JL powertrain 2.0T: 0W-20 full synthetic. 3.6L: 0W-20. Dana 44 (front and rear Rubicon): Mopar 75W-140 + friction modifier. Transfer case: ATF+4.
JL Field Verdict

The JL is the easiest Wrangler to trail-prep because it has fewer known failure points. Focus on your specific powertrain, carry the transfer case fuse and an OBDII scanner, and you’ve covered the serious risks. The soft top (if equipped) is the most common nuisance.
